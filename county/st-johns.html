Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Emergency Services & Homeless Coalition of St. Johns County — Shelter

    62 Chapin Street, St. Augustine, FL 32084
    (904) 819-0059
    Emergency shelter and transitional housing for families.
    https://homelesscoalitionstjohns.com

  • St. Francis House (St. Augustine)

    70 Washington Street, St. Augustine, FL 32084
    (904) 829-8937
    Shelter for homeless men and women, plus daily lunches. DISAMBIGUATION: this is a separate organization from "St. Francis House" in Gainesville (Alachua County) — different orgs, same common name. The Gainesville org's Main Street shelter closed permanently in March 2026, so this St. Augustine org (unaffected) is not a fallback for the same Union County gap anymore.

  • Alpha Omega Miracle Home

    2860 Collins Avenue, St. Augustine, FL 32084
    (904) 823-8588
    Housing for expectant/single mothers, children, and the elderly. Also operates a thrift store providing free clothing vouchers to homeless and poor families referred to the agency.
    https://www.aomh.org

  • Betty Griffin Center — Emergency Shelter

    Confidential location for resident safety — not publicly listed; pin shows downtown St. Augustine
    (904) 824-1555 (24-hour hotline)
    Domestic violence and sexual assault emergency shelter and transitional housing, counseling, and advocacy.
    https://bettygriffincenter.org

  • St. Gerard Campus

    1405 US Highway 1 South, St. Augustine, FL 32086
    (904) 829-5516
    Residential housing for pregnant/parenting teens ages 12-18, plus free pregnancy testing, sonograms, and counseling.
    https://stgerardcampus.org

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • Betty Griffin Center — 24-Hour DV Hotline

    Confidential location — 24-hour crisis hotline; pin shows downtown St. Augustine
    (904) 824-1555
    24-hour domestic violence and sexual assault crisis hotline, counseling, and advocacy.
    https://bettygriffincenter.org

  • 211 (St. Johns County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown St. Augustine
    211 or (904) 632-0600
    24/7 free information and referral line, part of United Way Northeast Florida's 9-county 211 network. Genuine gap fill — every other county on this map already had a dedicated 211 row, St. Johns was missing one despite having other hotline/food/medical entries.
    https://unitedwaynefl.org/get-help/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
