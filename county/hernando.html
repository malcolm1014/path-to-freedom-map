Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Jericho Road Ministries — Men's Shelter (Mondon Hill)

    1090 Mondon Hill Rd, Brooksville, FL
    (352) 799-2912
    Previously described (via third-party shelter directories, not the org's own site) as an emergency daily shelter for homeless men — ~14 beds, meals and clothing, entry point to Jericho Road's recovery program. See the idRequired staleness flag — verify this is still active and still low-barrier before counting on it for a same-night stay; Joshua's House below is the facility Jericho Road's own site currently describes for men.
    https://www.jericho-road.net

  • Jericho Road Ministries — Mary's House (Women's Shelter) & Jericho Food Barn

    1163 Howell Ave, Brooksville, FL 34601
    (352) 799-2912 ext 112 or 109
    Per third-party shelter directories: up to 3 days of emergency shelter for women (bed count varies by source, commonly cited as 6), after which clients must commit to one of Jericho Road's recovery programs to continue staying. The Jericho Food Barn pantry runs from the same campus, Tues & Fri 9am–noon (must have Hernando Co. ID for the FOOD PANTRY specifically — that ID requirement is well-sourced; it's the shelter side that's unconfirmed on the org's current site).
    https://www.jericho-road.net

  • Jericho Road Ministries — Joshua's House (REbuilt Recovery Program, men)

    11014 Broad St, Brooksville, FL 34601
    (352) 799-2912
    NOT a walk-in/same-night emergency shelter — this is Jericho Road's men's residential facility for the REbuilt recovery program, a 5-to-25-month structured journey (first 3 months education-focused, no outside employment permitted; by month 5 the goal is employment). Apply via the Recovery Program Request form at jericho-road.net/get-help, then a staff phone interview, then notification of a spot or waitlist. ~30 beds, a remodeled former church funded by a $400,000 grant. Free — funded by donors.
    https://www.jericho-road.net/recovery

  • Dawn Center of Hernando County — Domestic Violence Emergency Shelter

    Confidential location (Brooksville area) — call the hotline; pin shows city center only, not the shelter
    24-Hour Hotline (352) 686-8430 · Admin (352) 684-7191
    24-hour staffed emergency shelter for survivors of domestic/sexual violence and their children — food, clothing, hygiene items, advocacy, crisis counseling, safety planning. The shelter's physical location is intentionally not published, for resident safety — call the hotline, do not try to locate the building.
    https://www.dawncenter.org

  • New Beginnings Youth Shelter (ages 10–17)

    Confidential-in-practice — a published street address (Sheriff Mylander Way, Brooksville) would not resolve on two independent geocoders, so this pin is a generalized Brooksville city-center point rather than risk a wrong location for a minors' shelter
    (352) 540-6015
    24/7/365 emergency shelter for youth ages 10–17 who are runaway, homeless, locked out, or need crisis placement due to abuse/neglect/trauma. Free. Serves Hernando, Sumter, and Citrus counties. Call first — this is a crisis intake line, not a walk-in.
    https://yfainc.org/hernando

  • Esther's House (REbuilt Recovery Program, women)

    19384 Ingram St, Brooksville, FL 34601
    (352) 799-2912 ext 308
    30-bed women's residential facility for Jericho Road's REbuilt recovery program (5-to-25 months) — this is confirmed by name on the org's own current site as one of its two residential centers, opened 2025. Distinct from (and much longer-term than) Mary's House's up-to-3-day emergency shelter above. Apply the same way as Joshua's House: Recovery Program Request form → phone pre-screening → notification. Free. CONFIDENCE NOTE: extension 308 comes from a single directory source, not independently corroborated — the main line (352) 799-2912 is solidly confirmed, so start there and ask for Esther's House if the extension doesn't connect.
    https://www.jericho-road.net/recovery

  • The Life Center of Hernando

    Confidential residential program — no street address published by the org itself; pin shows Brooksville, the county seat
    (352) 597-0119
    Long-term transitional housing (not emergency shelter) for up to 8 young mothers, ages 18–25, and their preschool children at risk of homelessness — time to give birth, finish school, and become employed. Priority given to pregnant applicants and mothers with children under 5. Some third-party directories list a street address for this org; it is deliberately omitted here because the org's own site does not publish one.
    https://www.lifecenterofhernando.com

  • Hernando County Warming Center (seasonal, cold-weather nights only)

    The Enrichment Center, 800 John Gary Grubbs Blvd, Brooksville, FL 34601
    (352) 540-4338
    Opens only on cold-weather nights (announced ad hoc via county news releases) — 8pm to 8am, no meals or cots provided, pets not accepted. Free TheBus transport: pickup 8pm at the Walmart, 7305 Broad St, Brooksville, return drop-off 8am same spot. Call county HHS or watch hernandocounty.us news for open/close announcements.
    https://www.hernandocounty.us

  • M.O.V.E. (Ministry of Outreach and Volunteer Engagement) — Transitional Housing

    4134 Lamson Ave, Spring Hill, FL 34608
    (352) 296-3699
    Runs a Transitional Housing Program for people experiencing homelessness ("a safe, supportive, and structured environment... to rebuild their lives, access resources, and work toward permanent housing"), plus a food bank, a clothes bank, a tutoring program, and a senior wellness program (companionship, transportation, food access) all from the same org. Found via this map's second church/charity-research pass — a genuinely new find, not on the original PHP sheet or the congressional resource guide.
    https://moveoutreach.com

Legal public camping

  • Silver Lake Campground (Withlacoochee State Forest, Croom Tract)

    31475 Silver Lake Road, Brooksville, FL 34602
    (352) 797-4140
    Developed campground, 23 sites, $13.45/night (RV or tent) — full water/electric (30 & 50 amp) hookups at every site, a bathhouse with real showers, dump station, boat ramp, and Withlacoochee State Trail access. 3 sites (19–21) have enhanced accessibility. Reserve via ReserveAmerica (1-877-879-3859) or call the Visitor Center — cheapest legal, amenity-having overnight option in the county's public land system, and the shower access alone makes it worth knowing about even without an overnight stay in mind. DIRECTIONS: same general area as the Croom Tract primitive zones — via I-75 Exit 301, east on SR-50/US-98, north on Croom-Rital Rd; via Suncoast Parkway (589), north to SR-50, east through Brooksville, north on US-41 to Croom Rd (CR-480), then follow signage to Silver Lake Road.
    https://floridastateforests.reserveamerica.com/camping/silver-lake-campground/r/facilityDetails.do?contractCode=FLFS&parkId=1120180

  • Croom Tract Primitive Camp Zone East (free, hike-in)

    Trailhead: Tucker Hill Trailhead, 26578 Croom Rd, Brooksville, FL 34601 — camp zone is a ~0.5 mile hike in from the parking area, marked by yellow bands on trees
    (352) 797-4140
    No camping fee for a solo camper or small group (Authorization only required for groups of 6+ — see Visitor Center entry); a separate $2/person day-use entrance fee (or $45 annual day-use pass) may apply just to park at the Tucker Hill trailhead regardless of camping — unconfirmed whether that's waived for registered overnight campers, ask when you call. Hike-in/hike-out only — no vehicle access to the camp zone itself. No water, no restrooms, no amenities beyond occasional fire rings/picnic tables at existing sites. Only cooking fires permitted. This is the more accessible of the two primitive zones (0.5 mi vs. 5.25 mi hike). DIRECTIONS to Tucker Hill Trailhead — via I-75: Exit 301, head east on SR-50/US-98, turn north on Croom-Rital Rd, continue ~8.5 miles. Via Suncoast Parkway (589): take it north to SR-50, head east through Brooksville, then north on US-41 (Broad St) to Croom Rd (CR-480), turn right/east ~5 miles to the trailhead.
    https://floridastateforests.reserveamerica.com/camping/croom-tract-primitive-camp-zone-east/r/facilityDetails.do?contractCode=FLFS&parkId=1120165

  • Croom Tract Primitive Camp Zone West (free, hike-in)

    Trailhead: Tucker Hill Trailhead, 26578 Croom Rd, Brooksville, FL 34601 — camp zone is a ~5.25 mile hike in from the parking area
    (352) 797-4140
    Same free-camping/permit rules and possible $2/person day-use parking fee as the East zone, but a much longer hike in (5.25 miles) — realistically only workable with backpacking gear. Same DIRECTIONS to Tucker Hill Trailhead as the East zone entry (shared trailhead) — via I-75: Exit 301, head east on SR-50/US-98, turn north on Croom-Rital Rd, continue ~8.5 miles.
