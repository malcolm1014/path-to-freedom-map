Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Genesis House

    541 E New Haven Ave, Melbourne, FL 32901
    (321) 312-6062
    Emergency shelter for homeless women and their children, and for pregnant women, operating in Brevard County since 1977. Also offers counseling, personal-improvement classes, and healthcare access to residents. Open Tue/Wed 12-4pm, Thu-Sat 11am-5pm per the county's 2026 resource guide.
    https://www.genesishousefl.org/

  • Serene Harbor — Domestic Violence Shelter

    Confidential — pin generalized to Palm Bay city center for resident safety
    (321) 726-8282 (24-hour hotline, call or text)
    Brevard County's certified domestic violence center — shelter, counseling, case management, support groups, and children's programs, all free of charge. Participates in the state's Address Confidentiality Program. Shelter location is confidential; mailing address only is PO Box 100039, Palm Bay, FL 32910.
    https://www.sereneharbor.org/

  • Family Promise of Brevard

    114 1st St, Cocoa, FL 32922
    (321) 209-3391
    Housing stability program for families with children — shelter plus case management toward permanent housing. Mon-Fri 9am-5pm. URL FLAG (2026-09-17): the org's own domain (familypromiseofbrevard.org) has lapsed (disconnected Wix site) — linked to the national Family Promise affiliate directory page instead.
    https://familypromise.org/latest/affiliate/family-promise-of-brevard-inc/

  • HFH Supportive Housing

    1490 Dignity Cir, Cocoa, FL 32922
    (321) 639-0166
    Supportive/transitional housing for people experiencing homelessness. Mon-Fri 9am-5pm. GEOCODING GAP: this address (Orchid Lake community, west Cocoa) did not resolve on either the Census geocoder or Nominatim after multiple retries — pin is an approximate Cocoa-area placeholder, not the exact building; verify the address with the org before a trip.
    https://hfhsh.org/

  • Matthew's Hope Mobile Bus Shelter Program

    825 Forrest Ave, Cocoa, FL 32922 (program base — buses relocate nightly)
    (407) 905-9500
    Launched 2025 — retrofitted buses with private stacked sleeping pods (curtains, linens, charging ports) providing ~60 overnight shelter beds across 3 buses once fully rolled out, each staying at one nighttime location at least 3 nights running before moving. General population (not gender-restricted), but reservation-based and intended for people actively working a program toward housing, not pure emergency walk-in — call ahead.
    https://www.matthewshopeministries.org/

  • Brevard County Cold Night Shelter Network

    Rotating sites: Sharing Center of Central Brevard (113 Aurora St, Cocoa), Providence Connects (2745 S Babcock St, Melbourne), LifePointe Ministries (4220 S Hopkins Ave, Titusville), Matthew's Hope (825 Forrest Ave, Cocoa)
    Dial 211 to confirm nightly activation/site
    Seasonal, general-population emergency shelter — activates when temperature (or wind-chill) drops below approximately 45°F, coordinated by the county EOC across multiple rotating church/nonprofit sites. Not a year-round resource; call 211 or check local news on cold nights to confirm which sites are open that evening.
    https://nbbd.com/npr/nbchs/Homelessness/1603-cold-night-presentation.pdf

    (407) 905-9500

Legal public camping

  • Manatee Hammock Park and Campground

    7275 US Highway 1, Titusville, FL 32780
    (321) 264-5083
    County-run campground with real hot showers (recreation hall also has a small pool, laundry, and camp store), 147 full-hookup RV sites plus 30 tent sites on the Indian River Lagoon. Max stay 90 days (RV/camping units) or 14 days (tents). Two other Brevard County campgrounds also exist: Wickham Park (Melbourne) and Long Point Park (Melbourne Beach). GEOCODING NOTE: pin sourced from a matched street-address POI (bus stop at the same house number) rather than a direct address match — Census/Nominatim structured queries for the full address failed.
    https://www.brevardfl.gov/ParksAndRecreation/Campgrounds/ManateeHammockPark
