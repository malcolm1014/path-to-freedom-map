Hotlines & crisis lines

  • Women In Distress — 24-Hour Crisis Line

    24-hour crisis line — confidential shelter location, no public address; pin shows Fort Lauderdale
    (800) 500-1119
    English and Spanish. Local line (954) 761-1133.
    https://widbroward.org/

  • Broward Homeless Helpline (CEASIR)

    Phone-first Coordinated Entry intake line — no walk-in address; pin shows Fort Lauderdale
    (954) 563-4357
    Broward County's central Coordinated Entry and Assessment Screening, Information and Referral (CEASIR) line — nearly every shelter in this project's Broward data (CHAC, NHAC, SHAC, Salvation Army day respite) requires intake through this helpline or the TaskForce Outreach Team rather than a walk-in. Also activated for cold-weather and hurricane shelter information. Full directory of services also available by dialing 211.
    https://www.taskforceFL.org

  • 211 (Broward County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows Fort Lauderdale
    211 or (954) 537-0211
    24/7 free information and referral helpline — its own distinct county network (not the Treasure Coast one). Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 22 counties running now, this time despite an existing entry already referencing 211 in passing without a dedicated row. URL FLAG (2026-09-17): 211-broward.org now returns a broken SSL/TLS handshake (ERR_SSL_PROTOCOL_ERROR) in both Node and a real browser — switched to the parent org's main site.
    https://www.unitedwaybroward.org/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
