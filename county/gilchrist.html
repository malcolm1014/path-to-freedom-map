Legal public camping

  • Ruth B. Kirby Gilchrist Blue Springs State Park

    7450 NE 60th St, High Springs, FL 32643
    Tent camping ~$18/night plus a $6.70 reservation fee (no utility fee for tents; RV/cabin sites add a $7/night utility fee). Physically within Gilchrist County despite the High Springs mailing address (High Springs itself is in Alachua County).
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/ruth-b-kirby-gilchrist-blue-springs-state-park

  • Otter Springs Park & Campground

    6470 SW 80th Avenue, Trenton, FL 32693
    (352) 463-0800
    100+ full-hookup RV/tent sites plus cabins on a spring-fed river — a second real in-county camping option alongside Gilchrist Blue Springs. CONFIDENCE NOTE: multiple sources (official site + tourism board) show a stale 'Opening 5/1/2026' notice and the official site's SSL certificate is broken as of this check — call ahead to confirm current operating status before visiting.
    http://ottersprings.com/
