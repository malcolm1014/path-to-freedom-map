Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Lotus House

    217 NW 15th Street, Miami, FL 33136
    (305) 438-0556
    The largest shelter for women and children in the country, in Miami's Overtown neighborhood — emergency shelter plus wraparound health, wellness, and children's programming.
    https://www.lotushouse.org/

  • The Salvation Army of Miami-Dade

    1907 NW 38th Street, Miami, FL 33142
    (305) 637-6700
    236 beds across three lodges (women & families, single men, veterans and persons with HIV/AIDS) plus case management. Intake is referred through the Homeless Helpline: 1-877-994-4357.
    https://miami.salvationarmyflorida.org/

  • Better Way of Miami

    800 NW 28th Street, Miami, FL 33127
    (305) 634-3409
    Residential substance-abuse recovery and transitional housing, including tracks for veterans and people with HIV/AIDS. CARF-accredited, state-licensed.
    http://www.betterwaymiami.org/

  • Miami-Dade Certified Domestic Violence Shelters (SafeSpace network)

    Confidential locations countywide — call the hotline; pin shows county center only, not a shelter
    305-679-0303
    Four county-certified DV shelters (Safespace Empowerment Center, SafeSpace Central, SafeSpace South, SafeSpace North) — locations are intentionally confidential for resident safety, same as other DV shelters on this map. Call the 24/7 hotline, don't try to find a building. NOTE: this Miami-Dade "SafeSpace" county shelter network is an unrelated organization from the similarly-named SafeSpace nonprofit headquartered in Indian River County — same name, two different orgs, different counties.
    https://www.miamidade.gov/global/initiatives/domesticviolence/home.page

Food pantries & meals

  • Joshua's Heart Foundation

    2056 NE 155 Street, North Miami Beach, FL 33162
    (305) 944-4452
    Free grocery pickup Mondays & Thursdays 12-3pm, plus two Saturdays a month 10am until supplies run out.
    https://joshuasheart.org/

  • Farm Share — Homestead Warehouse

    14125 SW 320th St, Homestead, FL 33033
    (305) 246-9909
    South Dade's Farm Share distribution warehouse — free groceries, no ID/income documentation required. Farm Share also runs rotating pop-up distributions across Miami-Dade; check farmshare.org/food-distributions-florida for the current schedule.
    https://www.farmshare.org/

  • Feeding South Florida — Caleb Center Distribution Site

    5400 NW 22 Ave, Miami, FL 33142
    (954) 518-1857
    One of Feeding South Florida's regular Miami-Dade distribution sites — the region's main food bank runs a ROTATING distribution schedule across many sites; check feedingsouthflorida.org's Distribution Locator for the current date/location before a special trip.
    https://feedingsouthflorida.org/distribution_locator/

  • Community Outreach Resource Center Food Pantry

    824 W Palm Drive, Florida City, FL 33034
    South Dade food pantry, distinct geography (Florida City) from the county's central-Miami food resources — Miami-Dade's food category remains thin relative to its population even with this addition.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • City of Miami Beach — Homeless Outreach Team

    771 17th Street, Miami Beach, FL 33139
    (305) 604-4663
    Walk-in center for homeless residents — shelter placement, relocation assistance, ID replacement. A real geographic gap fill: every other entry on this map clusters in mainland Miami/Homestead, this is the first Miami Beach entry.
    https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/city-hall/housing-and-community-development/homeless-outreach/

  • Camillus House

    1603 NW 7th Ave, Miami, FL 33136
    (305) 374-1065
    Miami-Dade's largest single homeless services provider — emergency/transitional/permanent housing, meals, and an on-site clinic (Camillus Health Concern) all at one campus. On an average night houses roughly 1,000 people across its programs.
    https://www.camillus.org/

  • Chapman Partnership — Miami Homeless Assistance Center

    1550 N Miami Ave, Miami, FL 33136
    (305) 329-3000
    One of Miami-Dade's two Homeless Assistance Centers (800 beds combined with the Homestead site) — shelter, meals, case management, children's services, and job readiness under one roof.
    https://chapmanpartnership.org/

  • Chapman Partnership — Homestead Homeless Assistance Center

    28205 SW 124 Court, Homestead, FL 33033
    (305) 329-3000
    Chapman Partnership's South Dade campus — same multi-service model as the Miami site, serving the Homestead/South Dade area so residents don't have to travel to downtown Miami.
    https://chapmanpartnership.org/

  • Miami Rescue Mission (The Caring Place)

    3553 NW 50th St, Miami, FL 33142
    1-800-817-4673
    Center for Men and Center for Women & Children, a food warehouse, and an on-site health clinic — serving Miami-Dade since 1922. A sister campus serves Broward County separately.
    https://caringplace.org/

Clinics & health centers

  • Community Health of South Florida — Doris Ison Health Center

    10300 SW 216th St, Miami, FL 33190
    (305) 253-5100
    Sliding-scale FQHC — primary care, dental, and behavioral health regardless of insurance/immigration status. One of several CHI locations across Miami-Dade.
    https://chisouthfl.org/

  • Community Health of South Florida — South Miami Health Center

    6350 Sunset Dr, South Miami, FL 33143
    (786) 293-5500
    Same CHI network as the Doris Ison Health Center already on this map (13 locations across Miami-Dade and Monroe) — real geographic complement, covering the county's central/South Miami area rather than the far south end.
    https://chisouthfl.org/health-centers/south-miami-health-center/

  • Club Fellowship

    9827 E Hibiscus St, Palmetto Bay, FL 33157
    (305) 740-2420

    https://www.fellowshiphouse.org/programs-services/

  • The Key Clubhouse of Kendall

    9000 SW 137th Ave Suite 220, Miami, FL 33186
    (786) 504-4374
    Clubhouse-model recovery-through-work program for adults managing schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and other serious mental illness — opened July 2024 as this org's second South Florida site. Sister location: The Key Clubhouse of South Florida in Miami (also on this map).
    https://www.keyclubhouse.org/

  • The Key Clubhouse of South Florida

    8301 NW 27th Ave #102, Miami, FL 33147
    (305) 693-3508
    This org's original Miami location. Sister location: The Key Clubhouse of Kendall (also on this map).
    https://www.keyclubhouse.org/

Transportation

  • Miami-Dade Transit — Special Transportation Service (STS)

    701 NW 1st Court Suite 131, Miami, FL 33136
    786-469-5000
    ADA paratransit, $3.50/one-way trip, door-to-door, 24/7. Certified riders also get free Metrobus/Metrorail. Apply for a Special Transportation Service EASY Card to use it.
    https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1471890065439510

    12451 SW 184th St, Miami, FL 33177
    (305) 232-1049

    (305) 375-1490

    12000 Biscayne Blvd, North Miami, FL 33181
    211

    https://211miami.org/

    11285 SW 211 Street Suite 302, Miami, FL 33189
    (305) 438-2427
