Coordinating agencies

  • Doorways of Northwest Florida

    819 E. 11th Street, Panama City, FL 32401
    (850) 215-8800
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for a real 6-county region: Bay, Calhoun, Gulf, Holmes, Jackson, and Washington counties — coordinated entry point connecting people to housing, employment, healthcare, food, and transportation resources across all six.
    https://doorwaysnwfl.org/
