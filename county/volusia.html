  • 211 (Volusia County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown Daytona Beach
    211 or (386) 253-0564
    24/7 free information and referral line, "2-1-1 Volusia Flagler/First Call For Help" — same network already added for Flagler County. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern just caught in St. Johns and Flagler.
