Shelter (overnight beds)

  • The Path Rescue Mission (The Path of Citrus County)

    21 S Melbourne St, Beverly Hills, FL 34465
    (352) 527-6500 ext. 1
    Christian shelter for men, women, children, and families with no time limit — focus on rehabilitation, job re-training, case management, life skills. Walk-ins not accepted; call (352) 527-6500 and press 1, or text (352) 586-2611. Also publishes its own countywide resource guide (pathofcitrus.org) — a major source for this county's entries.
    https://www.pathofcitrus.org

  • Mission in Citrus — Crystal River

    2488 North Pennsylvania Ave, Crystal River, FL 34428
    (352) 794-3825
    Emergency shelter for men, women, and children, historically one of the only shelters in the county with no preconditions (no sobriety/income/marital-status requirement) and a resident-run model. CONFIDENCE NOTE: a July 2025 news report described the shelter facing closure and relocating ~30 residents; the org's own site was still live with no closure notice as of this pass (Aug 2026) and no 2026 news confirming either reopening or permanent closure was found — call ahead before relying on this as a same-night option.
    https://missionincitrus.com

  • Bridges 4 Veterans (Mission in Citrus — Inverness Veterans Shelter)

    306 South Park Ave, Inverness, FL 34450
    (352) 419-5451
    Veterans-specific shelter program, ~28 beds historically. Same parent org and closure-scare caveat as the Crystal River location above — call ahead.
    https://missionincitrus.com

    Confidential — shelter location not publicly listed for resident safety; pin is a generalized Inverness city-center point. PO Box 205, Inverness, FL 34451.

Legal public camping

  • Chassahowitzka River Campground (out of county, nearby)

    8600 W Miss Maggie Dr, Chassahowitzka, FL 34448
    (352) 382-2200
    Just across the Citrus County line, included because it's the nearest fee campground to the parts of Hernando County where the WMA itself (Chassahowitzka Wildlife Management Area, overnight camping NOT permitted there) is the closest public land. 53 full-hookup RV sites plus 28 primitive tent sites, boat ramp, general store. Call for current nightly rates — not listed publicly. The land is owned by SWFWMD; county management was briefly in question and reconfirmed via a new 40-year agreement (per a 2025-09-12 WUFT report), so it remains open and county-run as listed here.
    https://chassahowitzkaflorida.com

  • Fort Cooper State Park

    3100 S Old Floral City Rd, Inverness, FL 34450
    (352) 726-0315
    Primitive camping ($5/night) primarily used by cyclists on the adjoining Withlacoochee State Trail; day-use entry $3/vehicle. A second legal option alongside Chassahowitzka River Campground for this county.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/fort-cooper-state-park

Coordinating agencies

  • Mid Florida Homeless Coalition (Regional HQ — Continuum of Care Lead)

    1512 US-41, Inverness, FL 34450 (pin is Inverness city center — exact address didn't resolve on the geocoders used; street address is otherwise correct per the org's own site)
    (352) 860-2308
    HQ is in Citrus County, not Hernando, but this is the official HUD Continuum of Care lead agency for Citrus, Hernando, Lake, and Sumter counties — the entry point for coordinated-entry housing placement and diversion/wrap-around services. As of late-2025 reporting, MFHC's own leadership described Hernando County service capacity as "pretty narrow in scope" — meaning MFHC is the right place to call to get connected/assessed, but persistence and follow-up matter given limited local shelter capacity. Office hours Mon–Fri 8am–5pm.
    https://midfloridahomeless.org
