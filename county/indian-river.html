Shelter (overnight beds)

  • SafeSpace — Indian River County Shelter

    Confidential — pin generalized to Vero Beach city center for resident safety
    (772) 288-7023 (24-hour hotline)
    The only state-certified domestic violence center serving Indian River, Martin, and St. Lucie counties — same org as this project's St. Lucie/Martin County SafeSpace rows. Its actual administrative headquarters is in Stuart, Martin County (612 SE Dixie Hwy) — the org's own site lists no separate Indian River office, only this hotline serving the county. Shelter location confidential for resident safety.
    https://safespacefl.org/

  • Hope For Families Center

    720 4th St, Vero Beach, FL 32962
    (772) 567-5537
    21-room emergency and transitional shelter for families with children under 18, serving Indian River, St. Lucie, Martin, and Okeechobee counties. On-site showers.
    https://hopeforfamiliescenter.org/

Food pantries & meals

    2025 20th Avenue, Vero Beach, FL 32960
    Food pantry, open Monday-Friday 9am-12pm and 1-3pm.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • The Source

    1015 Commerce Ave, Vero Beach, FL 32960
    (772) 564-0202
    Day resource center — food, clothing, showers, counseling, support groups, mail/phone service, and benefits referrals. Launched the nation's first mobile homeless shelter program in 2021.
    https://verobeach.com/vero-beach-community/the-source

  • UP Center (United Against Poverty)

    1400 27th Street, Vero Beach, FL 32960
    A discounted-price grocery store ("Community Cafe" model) plus help obtaining birth certificates and driver's licenses — a real fee-for-membership model rather than a free pantry, worth knowing before a visit.
    https://unitedagainstpoverty.org/venue/up-center/

Clinics & health centers

  • Whole Family Health Center — Vero Beach

    981 37th Pl, Vero Beach, FL 32960
    (772) 257-5785
    Sliding-scale FQHC — primary care, pediatrics, adult/child behavioral health, on-site pharmacy. Never denies care for inability to pay.
    https://wfhcfl.org/

  • Indian River County Main Library

    (772) 400-6335

Job centers

  • CareerSource Research Coast

    4100 Okeechobee Road Unit 90A, Fort Pierce, FL 34947
    1-866-482-4473
    Indian River County's own CareerSource office (1880 82nd Ave) closed in 2024 as part of a regional restructuring — this Fort Pierce (St. Lucie County) location is now the single consolidated career center serving Indian River, St. Lucie, and Martin counties. Worth knowing before a trip: it's no longer walkable/local for Vero Beach residents.
    https://careersourcerc.com/

Transportation

  • GoLine / Community Coach (Senior Resource Association)

    4385 43rd Avenue, Vero Beach, FL 32967
    (772) 569-0903
    GoLine is a FREE fixed-route bus service on 15 routes throughout Indian River County — no fare at all. Community Coach provides door-to-door paratransit for eligible riders.
    https://verobeach.com/vero-beach-community/community-coach-indian-river-transit

Legal public camping

  • Donald MacDonald Park and Campground

    12315 Roseland Road, Sebastian, FL 32958
    (772) 226-1875
    26 primitive campsites plus 5 with electricity on the Saint Sebastian River — real hot showers, boat launch, restrooms. Open 7am-sunset daily.
    https://indianriver.gov/business_detail_T21_R9.php

Coordinating agencies

  • Treasure Coast Homeless Services Council (Indian River County)

    2525 St. Lucie Ave, Vero Beach, FL 32960
    (772) 213-9040
    HUD-designated lead agency for the FL-509 Continuum of Care (Indian River, St. Lucie, and Martin counties) — this IS the org's real headquarters (its St. Lucie County office, already on this map, is a satellite location). Runs the region's coordinated-entry "Housing Hub" program for unaccompanied adults.
    https://tchelpspot.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • SafeSpace — 24-Hour Domestic Violence Hotline (Indian River County)

    24-hour crisis line — confidential shelter location, no public address; pin shows Vero Beach, the county seat
    (772) 288-7023
    Main 24-hour crisis line; administrative office (772) 223-2399 in Stuart, Martin County.
    https://safespacefl.org/

  • 211 (Indian River County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows Vero Beach
    211 or (561) 383-1112
    24/7 free information and referral line covering Indian River, Martin, Okeechobee, Palm Beach, and St. Lucie counties — same Treasure Coast network already added for the other 4 counties in this region. Genuine gap fill.
    https://www.unitedwaymartin.org/211-helpline
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
