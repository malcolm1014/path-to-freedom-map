Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Compassion House — The Salvation Army

    821 SE Martin Luther King Jr Blvd, Stuart, FL 34994
    (772) 288-1471
    Transitional shelter for women and women with children, typical stay 3-4 months; private bedroom/bathroom per family. Same regional org as this project's St. Lucie and Okeechobee County Salvation Army entries (Salvation Army of Martin, St. Lucie & Okeechobee Counties).

  • Mary's Home of the Treasure Coast

    1033 E 14th St, Stuart, FL 34996
    (772) 223-5000
    The only transitional maternity home in Martin County — residential and non-residential support, care, and counseling for pregnant women in unstable housing situations.
    https://www.maryshome.org/

  • M.I.S.S. Inc. of the Treasure Coast

    3820 SE Dixie Highway, Stuart, FL 34997
    (772) 781-4063
    This is the org's actual home address — a cross-county reference row already exists in this project's St. Lucie County data ("M.I.S.S. Inc. of the Treasure Coast (St. Lucie County)") since the org explicitly serves both counties from this one Stuart office; this is the native Martin County row.
    https://www.missinc.org/

  • SafeSpace — Martin County Shelter

    Confidential — pin generalized to Stuart city center for resident safety
    (772) 288-7023 (24-hour hotline)
    The only state-certified domestic violence center serving Indian River, Martin, and St. Lucie counties — same org as this project's St. Lucie County SafeSpace rows. Shelter location confidential for resident safety.
    https://safespacefl.org/

Food pantries & meals

  • House of Hope of Martin County

    2484 SE Bonita Street, Stuart, FL 34997
    (772) 286-4673
    Client-choice food pantry plus clothing, furniture, financial assistance, and case management. Mon-Fri 9:30am-4:30pm (closed 12:30-1pm), Wed 12-4:30pm. Also runs Hobe Sound, Indiantown, and Jensen Beach service centers not yet individually mapped — a candidate for this project's phase-2 deepening sweep.
    https://www.hohmartin.org/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Love and Hope in Action (LAHIA)

    1760 SE Salerno Road, Stuart, FL 34997
    (772) 781-7002
    Full daytime safe haven — nutrition assistance, clothing, bedding, camping supplies, bicycles, replacement ID help, mail service, transportation, and financial-assistance referrals, all free. Showers and laundry split into a separate hygiene row below.
    https://www.lahia.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • SafeSpace — 24-Hour Domestic Violence Hotline (Martin County)

    24-hour crisis line — confidential shelter location, no public address; pin shows Stuart, the county seat
    (772) 288-7023
    Main 24-hour crisis line; administrative office (772) 223-2399. Note: this project's St. Lucie County SafeSpace hotline row lists a different number (772-464-4555), described in that search result specifically as the St. Lucie County line — both appear to be genuine active SafeSpace numbers reaching the same regional org, surfaced here rather than silently picking one.
    https://safespacefl.org/

  • 211 (Martin County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows Stuart
    211 or (561) 383-1112
    24/7 free information and referral line covering Indian River, Martin, Okeechobee, Palm Beach, and St. Lucie counties — same Treasure Coast network already added for Okeechobee and St. Lucie. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 20 counties running now.
    https://www.unitedwaymartin.org/211-helpline
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
