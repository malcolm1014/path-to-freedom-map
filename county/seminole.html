Legal public camping

  • Wekiwa Springs State Park

    1800 Wekiwa Circle, Apopka, FL 32712 (physically located in Orange County)
    (407) 553-4383
    GENUINE GAP: no state park, state forest, or county park offering general camping was found physically located in Seminole County this pass — Wekiwa Springs' own park entrance is in Orange County (its springs are popularly associated with Seminole due to the Wekiva River corridor, but the park boundary itself is not) and Seminole State Forest, despite its name, is physically in Lake County.
