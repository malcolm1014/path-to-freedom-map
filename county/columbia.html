Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Another Way Shelter — Lake City

    496 SW Ring Ct, Lake City, FL 32025 (main office — actual shelter location confidential for resident safety)
    Hotline (866) 875-7983 · Outreach (386) 719-2700
    Domestic violence, sexual violence, human trafficking, and rape crisis services for male and female victims and their dependent children — serves Columbia, Hamilton, Suwannee, and Lafayette counties. This is the org's flagship location ("Another Way Shelter — Lake City" is literally its own name for this office) — same org as this project's Levy/Gilchrist/Dixie/Suwannee County entries.
    https://anotherwayinc.org

  • Renewed — Men's Shelter

    225 NW Real Terrace, Lake City, FL 32055
    (386) 438-5597
    A general-population men's shelter — same building/org as the hub entry already on this map, split into its own row. Fills a real gap: this county's only other prior shelter entry (Another Way) is DV-specific.

Food pantries & meals

  • Renewed — Meals & Food Pantry

    225 NW Real Terrace, Lake City, FL 32055
    (386) 438-5597
    Hot meals and food pantry, Mon/Wed/Fri 12-3pm — same building/org as the hub entry already on this map, split into its own row. A real second food option alongside Lad Soup Kitchen.

  • Lad Soup Kitchen

    127 NE Escambia Street, Lake City, FL 32025
    (386) 758-2217
    All meals free, donations accepted. Breakfast Mon–Fri 7:30–9am; lunch Mon–Fri 12–2pm, Sat 12–3pm; Sun 1–3:30pm — one of the most extensive free daily meal schedules found in this project so far.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Renewed

    225 NW Real Terrace, Lake City, FL 32055
    (386) 438-5597
    A major multi-service hub: hot meals (Mon/Wed/Fri 12–3pm), showers, hygiene bags, a thrift store (Mon–Fri 10am–5pm, Sat 10am–2pm), a men's shelter, free income-based counseling, computer/job-search classes (last Thursday of the month), and "Genesis" drug and alcohol rehabilitation support (Thursdays). This project's Suwannee County entry referenced this same org as an out-of-county fallback for hygiene — this is its actual home location.

  • Catholic Charities — Lake City

    553 NW Railroad Street, Lake City, FL 32055
    Admin (386) 754-9180 · Food Bank (386) 755-5683
    Food, financial services, case management, family/individual counseling, prescription assistance. Admin Mon–Fri 7:30am–3:30pm; food bank Mon–Fri 7:30–11:30am (check in at front office). Serves Columbia, Hamilton, Lafayette, and Suwannee counties.

  • North Star Family Resource Center

    255 NE Coach Anders Lane, Lake City, FL 32055
    (386) 758-3319
    Family-focused community hub: notary services, homework help, faxing, computer use, job and resource boards, a clothing closet, an emergency food pantry, and self-sufficiency services. Mon–Thu 9am–5pm, Fri 9am–1pm.

Legal public camping

  • O'Leno State Park Campground

    410 SE O'Leno Park Rd, High Springs, FL 32643
    (386) 454-1853
    61 campsites with water/electric hookups across two loops, family camping ~$18/night (up to 8 people, 2 tents/vehicles per site). Along the Santa Fe River, 6 miles north of High Springs — physically within Columbia County despite the High Springs mailing address.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/oleno-state-park
