Shelter (overnight beds)

  • C.A.R.E. (Center for Abuse & Rape Emergencies) — Office

    1501 Cooper Street, Punta Gorda, FL 33951 (administrative office — the actual Safe Haven emergency shelter location is confidential for resident safety)
    (941) 627-6000
    Domestic violence and sexual assault shelter (Safe Haven) and services for Charlotte County. Also has an annex at 6868 San Casa Dr, Englewood.
    https://carefl.org

  • Charlotte CARE Center

    1476 Kenesaw Street, Port Charlotte, FL 33952
    (941) 627-4313
    Low-barrier emergency shelter (up to 30 days), open 365 days/year. Successor organization to the former Charlotte County Homeless Coalition — functions as the county's primary point of entry for homeless services, alongside a food pantry (Wed 1-4:30pm, Fri 10am-1pm) and rapid-rehousing homeless-prevention program. Split into shelter, hub, and food rows in this data.
    https://www.svdpsp.org/programs/homeless-services/charlotte-care-center/

Coordinating agencies

  • Gulf Coast Partnership

    21500 Gibralter Drive, Suite 3, Port Charlotte, FL 33952
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for Charlotte County — coordinated entry access point for homeless/housing-crisis services. One of only 5 U.S. communities recognized by USICH/HUD for effectively ending veteran (2017) and chronic (2021) homelessness.
    https://www.gulfcoastpartnership.org

Hotlines & crisis lines

    1501 Cooper Street, Punta Gorda, FL 33951 (administrative office — hotline is 24/7 regardless of office hours)
    (941) 627-6000
    24-hour domestic violence and sexual assault crisis hotline for Charlotte County.

  • 211 (Charlotte County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows Punta Gorda
    211 or (941) 627-3539
    24/7 free information and referral line — a distinct 211 network from the Suncoast (Manatee/Sarasota) and other regional operators. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 10 counties running now.
    https://unitedwayccfl.org
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
