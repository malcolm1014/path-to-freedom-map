Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Waterfront Rescue Mission

    348 West Herman St, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 436-7868
    Escambia County's main emergency shelter for men and women (separate check-in windows). Also runs a 90-day Life Builder Recovery substance-abuse program on-site, and a clothing-assistance program at this same address for flood/fire victims (referral required).
    https://waterfrontmission.org/

  • FavorHouse of Northwest Florida

    2001 West Blount St, Pensacola, FL 32501 (intervention office; shelter location confidential)
    (850) 434-6600 (24-hour crisis hotline)
    Certified domestic violence center serving Escambia and Santa Rosa counties — intervention office Mon-Fri 8:30am-5pm, confidential emergency shelter, services in English and Spanish.
    https://www.favorhouse.org/

  • Heavenly Blessing

    1614 North T St, Pensacola, FL 32505
    (850) 341-5039
    Emergency shelter for men and women, open 24/7 — one of the few genuinely walk-in-friendly shelter options in the county's own resource guide.

  • Currie House

    4610 West Fairfield Dr, Pensacola, FL 32506
    (850) 453-2772
    Youth shelter for ages 10-17, run by Children's Home Society.

  • Loaves and Fishes

    257 East Lee St, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 438-7616
    Shelter for families with children, and for pregnant women or women with children.

Food pantries & meals

  • St. Vincent de Paul Society

    5200 Saufley Field Rd, Pensacola, FL 32526
    (850) 476-0690
    Food pantry, Tue & Thu 10am-12pm. A second St. Vincent de Paul pantry location operates at 2200 West De Soto St, Pensacola, Tue 10am-2pm.

  • Alfred Washburn Center — Meals

    31 Murphy Ln, Pensacola, FL 32505
    (850) 637-1654
    Meals served Mon, Wed-Sat 8:30am-1pm — same org/address as the showers entry already on this map, split into its own row. A real second food option alongside St. Vincent de Paul Society.

  • Samaritan Hands Ministry — Food Pantry

    511 Tarragona St, Pensacola, FL 32502
    (850) 432-3333
    Food pantry, Tue-Thu 9am-11:30am — same org/address as the clothing entry already on this map, split into its own row. A real third food option in Escambia County.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Overflow Health Alliance

    2200 North Palafox St, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 432-3333
    Multi-service hub — food pantry (Mon-Fri 9am-4pm, Sat 9am-12pm), rent/financial assistance (call for appointment, no walk-ins), free HIV testing, and a transitional housing program (Transition Home) for men and women — no emergency/walk-in shelter beds, transitional only.

Clinics & health centers

  • Florida Department of Health — Escambia County

    1295 West Fairfield Dr, Pensacola, FL 32501
    Call for appointment line
    County health department's main clinic — WIC, immunizations, HIV/AIDS services (ADAP), pregnancy support (Improved Pregnancy Outcome Program), Mon-Fri 8am-5pm.
    https://escambia.floridahealth.gov/

  • Health and Hope Clinic

    1718 East Olive Rd, Pensacola, FL 32514
    Book online at healthandhopeclinic.org
    Sliding-scale/free clinic — Mon, Wed, Fri 8:30am-1pm, Tue & Thu 8:30am-8pm.
    https://www.healthandhopeclinic.org/

Clothing & thrift vouchers

  • Samaritan Hands Ministry

    511 Tarragona St, Pensacola, FL 32502
    (850) 432-3333
    Clothing assistance and a food pantry, Tue-Thu 9am-11:30am — one of the county's few resources that isn't strictly appointment-only.

Showers (gyms)

  • Alfred Washburn Center — Showers

    31 Murphy Ln, Pensacola, FL 32505
    (850) 637-1654
    A real dedicated shower resource (not just a gym membership workaround) — Mon, Wed-Sat 10am-1pm. Also serves meals Mon, Wed-Sat 8:30am-1pm.

Free computers & workspace

  • West Florida Public Libraries — Pensacola Library

    239 North Spring St, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 436-5060
    Main branch of Escambia County's library system, Mon-Fri 10am-7pm, Sat 10am-4pm. Free computer/internet access. Other branches: Bellview, Brownsville, Century, Molino, Southwest, Tryon, and Westside.
    https://www.mywfpl.com/

Job centers

  • CareerSource EscaRosa — Pensacola

    6913 North 9th Ave, Pensacola, FL 32504
    (850) 607-8700
    Job search, resume help, veterans employment assistance (DVOP/LVER specialists). Other locations in Milton and Century (Santa Rosa/Escambia border area) also serve this region.
    https://www.careersourceescarosa.com/

Government benefits offices

  • ACCESS Florida — Escambia County

    1301 North 9th Ave, Pensacola, FL 32503
    (850) 300-4323
    SNAP, Medicaid, and cash assistance applications, Mon-Fri 8am-5pm.
    https://www.myflorida.com/accessflorida

    1515 West Fairfield Dr, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 595-3228

Pet food pantries

  • Pensacola Humane Society — Pet Food Pantry

    5 N Q St, Pensacola, FL 32505
    Call for current info
    Free pet food and supplies for those who temporarily can't feed their pet — 2nd and 4th Wednesdays, 10am-1pm.
    https://pensacolahumane.org/

  • Escambia County Animal Shelter

    200 W Fairfield Dr, Pensacola, FL 32501
    (850) 595-3900
    Pet adoption, licensing, animal control, and fostering — the county's official shelter, distinct from the nonprofit Pensacola Humane Society's own pet-food program.
    https://myescambia.com/our-services/animal-welfare/animal-shelter

Legal public camping

  • Fort Pickens Campground (Gulf Islands National Seashore)

    1400 Fort Pickens Road, Pensacola Beach, FL 32561
    (850) 934-2600
    177 sites (137 electric/water hookup, 40 tent-only) with real heated showers and flush toilets. Reservations via recreation.gov, up to 6 months ahead.
    https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234704

Coordinating agencies

  • Opening Doors of Northwest Florida

    1020 North New Warrington Rd, Pensacola, FL 32506
    (850) 439-3009
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for Escambia and Santa Rosa counties — evolved from the EscaRosa Coalition on Homelessness (founded 1986). Also runs direct outreach services (birth certificates, IDs, employment/housing/mental-health/healthcare referrals), Mon-Thu 8-11:30am & 1:30-3pm.
    https://openingdoorsnwfl.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • 211 Northwest Florida

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Pensacola, the county seat
    Dial 211 · (855) 420-8689
    24/7 information and referral for Escambia and Santa Rosa counties, run by United Way of West Florida. Also the intake line for the Florida Veterans Support Line (dial 211 ext. 5).
    https://211nwfl.org/

  • FavorHouse — 24-Hour Crisis Hotline

    Phone service — pin shows Pensacola, the county seat
    (850) 434-6600
    24-hour crisis line for domestic violence and sexual assault survivors — same org as this map's FavorHouse shelter entry.
    https://www.favorhouse.org/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
