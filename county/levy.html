Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Another Way, Inc. (Levy County)

    Confidential — shelter location not publicly listed for resident safety; pin is a generalized Bronson (county seat) point.
    (866) 875-7983 (24-hour helpline)
    Domestic violence & rape crisis center serving Columbia, Dixie, Gilchrist, Hamilton, Lafayette, Levy, and Suwannee counties — same confidential-location pattern as this project's other DV shelters. GENUINE GAP: no general-population emergency homeless shelter (overnight beds, no DV/recovery-program restriction) was found physically located in Levy County this pass — only hurricane/weather shelters (schools, opened on an as-needed basis) turned up in searches. This DV shelter is the only standing shelter-category resource found; documented as a real service gap for this rural county, not an oversight.
    https://anotherwayinc.org
