Shelter (overnight beds)

  • The Salvation Army — Manatee County

    1204 14th Street West, Bradenton, FL 34205
    (941) 748-5110
    Emergency overnight shelter, case management, adult rehabilitation, community dinner, cold-weather shelters, and financial assistance for utility/rental payments.
    https://bradenton.salvationarmyflorida.org/

  • Family Resources — Safe Place 2B

    1001 9th Avenue West, Bradenton, FL 34205
    (941) 708-5850
    Youth homeless shelter for teens, plus counseling, anger-management, and anti-bullying services.
    https://www.familyresourcesinc.org

  • HOPE Family Services — Emergency Shelter

    Confidential location for resident safety — not publicly listed; pin shows downtown Bradenton
    Domestic violence emergency shelter and services for Manatee County.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Turning Points

    701 17th Avenue West, Bradenton, FL 34205
    (941) 747-1509
    Day resource center — food pantry (Our Daily Bread), hot meals, financial and housing assistance, employment services (partnered with Goodwill), veterans' services, mail service, and free medical/dental care. Also offers showers and laundry (see hygiene category).
    https://www.tpmanatee.org

  • Florida Dept. of Health in Manatee County

    410 6th Avenue East, Bradenton, FL 34208
    https://manatee.floridahealth.gov

  • MCR Health — East Manatee Health & Wellness Center

    1312 Manatee Ave E, Bradenton, FL 34208
    (941) 708-8500

Mental & behavioral health

  • Academy at Bradenton

    601 12th St W, Bradenton, FL 34205
    (941) 929-2800
    Clubhouse-model program for adults pursuing mental health wellness — vocational skill training, wellness activities, and career placement. Sister location to Academy at Glengary in Sarasota (also on this map); membership covers both sites. Founding network partner of Fountain House United.
    https://www.academysrq.org/en/bradenton/

Legal public camping

  • Lake Manatee State Park

    20007 State Road 64 East, Bradenton, FL 34212
    60 campsites with water and 30-amp electric hookups, 15 miles east of Bradenton. DISAMBIGUATION: not to be confused with Manatee Springs State Park, a differently-named park physically located in Levy County — this is the correct in-county park for Manatee County.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/lake-manatee-state-park

Coordinating agencies

  • Suncoast Partnership to End Homelessness

    1750 17th Street, Suite K-1, Sarasota, FL 34234 (physically located in Sarasota County)
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for the joint FL-506 CoC region (Manatee and Sarasota counties) — office sits in Sarasota County, same cross-county-coalition pattern used elsewhere in this project (e.g. Volusia/Flagler Coalition, Homeless Services Network of Central Florida).
    https://www.mymanatee.org/connect/resources/external-agencies/external-agency-detail/suncoast-partnership

  • 211 (Manatee County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown Bradenton
    211
    24/7 free information and referral line covering Manatee and Sarasota counties — a third distinct 211 network, different from both Orlando-metro and Polk/Hardee/Highlands. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 8 counties running now.

Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
