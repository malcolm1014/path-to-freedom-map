Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Caring Center

    1016 Main Street, Palatka, FL 32177
    (386) 328-1088
    Free emergency shelter for men, no charge for up to 3 days, one meal a day, and job-search assistance. One of two related properties in Palatka (see New Creation Christian Center).

  • New Creation Christian Center

    1406 Oak Street, Palatka, FL 32177
    (386) 328-1829
    Homeless shelter with support services and counseling specifically for individuals coming out of jail or prison.

  • Lee Conlee House

    PO Box 2558, Palatka, FL 32178 (confidential — actual shelter location not publicly listed for resident safety; pin shows Palatka, the county seat)
    (386) 325-3141 (24-hour hotline)
    Domestic-violence emergency shelter, 24-hour hotline, counseling, support groups, and full-service victim/court advocacy. Also runs The Shoppe thrift store (see Clothing category).
    https://leeconleehouse.org

Legal public camping

  • Rodman Campground

    108 Rodman Dam Road, Palatka, FL 32177
    (800) 326-3521 (reservations)
    64 sites in two loops: 26 primitive tent-only sites (~$12/night) and 38 sites with electric/water hookups (~$22/night plus fees). About 9 miles southwest of Palatka on the Rodman Reservoir. Open 8am until sundown, 365 days a year.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/rodman-campground
