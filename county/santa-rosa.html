Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Ferris Hill Baptist Church — Cold Weather Shelter

    6848 Chaffin Street, Milton, FL 32570
    (850) 479-4391
    Seasonal, cold-weather-only emergency shelter, activates when temperature drops below 40°F. GENUINE GAP: the county's own official 211 resource guide lists no year-round general-population shelter physically located in Santa Rosa County — this cold-weather overflow is the only shelter resource listed for the whole county outside of DV/out-of-county referrals.

  • FavorHouse of Northwest Florida (Santa Rosa County)

    2001 West Blount St, Pensacola, FL 32501 (physically located in Escambia County; shelter location confidential)
    (850) 434-6600 (24-hour crisis hotline)
    Certified domestic violence center explicitly serving both Escambia and Santa Rosa counties from one confidential shelter — same org already on this map for Escambia County.
    https://www.favorhouse.org/

  • Waterfront Rescue Mission (out of county, Pensacola) — Santa Rosa County

    348 West Herman St, Pensacola, FL 32501 (physically located in Escambia County)
    (850) 436-7868
    Since Santa Rosa County's own official resource guide lists no in-county general-population emergency shelter, this is the nearest real option — same org already on this map's Escambia County entries. Also runs fire-victims-only clothing assistance at 4467 Avalon Blvd, Milton, and 4376 Gulf Breeze Pkwy, Gulf Breeze.
    https://waterfrontmission.org/

Clinics & health centers

  • Santa Rosa Medical Center

    6002 Berryhill Road, Milton, FL 32570
    (850) 626-7762
    129-bed general hospital, the county's main acute-care facility with a 24/7 emergency department.
    https://www.srmcfl.com/

  • We Care — Santa Rosa County

    5527 Stewart St, Milton, FL 32570
    (850) 983-5200
    Established 2001 — connects uninsured adults with volunteer specialty physicians for uncompensated care. Same address as the county health department. CONFIDENCE NOTE: a separate Navarre-based charity also uses the name 'We Care' in the county's 211 guide for food/utility assistance (850-939-1005) — different org, same name, not to be confused with this DOH program.
    https://santarosa.floridahealth.gov/programs-and-services/clinical-and-nutrition-services/we-care/
