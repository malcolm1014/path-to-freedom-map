Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Christian Care Center — Samaritan Inn

    108 N. 14th St, Leesburg, FL 34748
    (352) 314-8733
    Multi-program shelter campus: Pregnancy & Family Care Center, a Children's Shelter, the Samaritan Inn (families), and a Men's Residence for alcohol/drug recovery. Also provides food, clothing, and household items for Lake County residents. Mon–Thu 8am–5pm. A second Christian Care Center office/pantry is listed at 1308 W. Main St, Leesburg — likely the same org's separate service window, not independently confirmed.
    https://www.christiancarecenter.org

  • New Beginnings of Central Florida

    415 Citrus Tower Blvd, Clermont, FL 34711
    (352) 404-6946
    Recovery-focused shelter program — "helping homeless learn to live a better life by making good choices and to recover from their hurts, habits and hang-ups."

  • Haven of Lake and Sumter Counties (Lake County)

    Confidential — shelter location not publicly listed for resident safety; pin is a generalized Tavares (county seat) point.
    (352) 753-5800 (24-hour helpline)
    24-hour crisis intervention and emergency shelter for victims of domestic and sexual violence and their children, serving both Lake and Sumter counties — same org as this project's Sumter County entry (renamed with a county suffix here to avoid a duplicate-name collision in the map data, not two different organizations).

    115 Citrus Avenue, Eustis, FL 32726
    (352) 589-8157

Legal public camping

  • Lake Louisa State Park Campground

    7305 US Highway 27, Clermont, FL 34714
    (352) 394-3969
    60-site full-facility campground, tent sites from ~$24/night, reservations via ReserveAmerica. The most accessible legal camping option in southern Lake County.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/lake-louisa-state-park
