Shelter (overnight beds)

  • St. Matthew's House

    2001 Airport Road South, Naples, FL 34112 (pin approximate — exact street number did not resolve on Census or Nominatim geocoders, road-level coordinate used instead; a real, documented geocoding gap, not a guess)
    (239) 774-0500
    124 shelter beds in Naples (operating since 1988) plus 44 more at its Immokalee Friendship House. Feeding ministry, thrift store (see clothing category), and drop-in showers (see hygiene category).
    https://stmatthewshouse.org

  • The Shelter for Abused Women & Children

    2635 Weeks Avenue, Naples, FL 34112
    Two 60-bed emergency shelters (Naples and Immokalee) plus nine transitional living cottages in Naples.
    https://naplesshelter.org

  • Immokalee Friendship House (St. Matthew's House)

    602 West Main Street, Immokalee, FL 34142
    (239) 657-4090
    44-bed shelter serving the Immokalee farmworker community — warm showers, hot meals, laundry, and case management, open 24/7.
    https://stmatthewshouse.org
