  • The Salvation Army — Shelter (Fort Myers)

    2400 Edison Avenue, Fort Myers, FL 33901
    (239) 334-3745
    Shelter for women and families, Rapid Rehousing program. Access through 211.
    https://salvationarmyflorida.org

  • ACT (Abuse Counseling & Treatment) — Shelter

    Confidential location for resident safety — not publicly listed; pin shows downtown Fort Myers
    (239) 939-3112 (24-hour crisis line)

    https://www.actabuse.com

  • We Care Outreach (Hanna House / Eternal Home)

    4231 Desoto Avenue, Fort Myers, FL 33905
    (239) 693-7911
    Men's/women's/family recovery transitional housing, food pantry and clothing closet, showers, case management, and an ACCESS Florida assistance site all at one location.
    https://wecarefortmyers.org/

  • 211 (Lee County)
