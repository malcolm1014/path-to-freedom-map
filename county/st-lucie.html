Shelter (overnight beds)

  • GraceWay Village Family Shelter

    1780 Hartman Rd, Fort Pierce, FL 34947
    (772) 925-3074
    St. Lucie County's first dedicated family homeless shelter, built in phases at GraceWay Village's existing campus — 10 private family units, classrooms, laundry. The Treasure Coast Homeless Services Council also operates a separate, unaccompanied-adults "Housing Hub" (men's and women's branches) — no public street address was found for that facility after searching; contact TCHSC (see coalition row) for access.
    https://gracewayvillage.com/

  • SafeSpace — St. Lucie County Shelter

    Confidential — pin generalized to Fort Pierce city center for resident safety
    (772) 464-4555 (24-hour hotline)
    The only state-certified domestic violence center serving Indian River, Martin, and St. Lucie counties. Shelter location is confidential for resident safety, same pattern as this project's other DV-shelter entries.
    https://safespacefl.org/

  • M.I.S.S. Inc. of the Treasure Coast (St. Lucie County)

    3820 SE Dixie Highway, Stuart, FL 34997 (physically located in Martin County)
    (772) 781-4063
    Transitional housing and support for homeless women with children, pregnant women, women with mental health needs, or single seniors — explicitly serves Martin AND St. Lucie counties from its Stuart office. Cross-county reference row (same pattern as this project's Heartland Coalition/CareerSource entries) so a St. Lucie-filtered map view isn't shelter-empty; expect a native Martin County row when that county gets its own pass.
    https://www.missinc.org/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • UP St. Lucie Center (United Against Poverty)

    2520 Orange Avenue, Fort Pierce, FL 34947
    (772) 468-8543
    Choice-model grocery store plus career-readiness/financial-mobility programs. Florida's DCF co-locates ACCESS (SNAP/Medicaid/TANF) caseworkers on-site — see the separate St. Lucie County ACCESS Center row at this same address; two real, distinct services sharing one building, not a duplicate entry.
    https://unitedagainstpoverty.org/campus/st-lucie/

  • GraceWay Village

    1780 Hartman Rd, Fort Pierce, FL 34947
    (772) 925-3074
    Hot meals (Matthew's Café, Sunday PM), clothing for children/teens in crisis, adult education, and referral services. Building St. Lucie County's first dedicated family homeless shelter on this same campus — see the separate shelter row.
    https://gracewayvillage.com/

Legal public camping

  • Savannas Recreation Area

    1400 Midway Road, Fort Pierce, FL 34982
    (772) 464-7855

Coordinating agencies

  • Treasure Coast Homeless Services Council

    609 North 7th Street, Fort Pierce, FL 34950
    (772) 213-9040
    HUD-designated lead agency for the FL-509 Continuum of Care (Indian River, St. Lucie, and Martin counties). This is a real St. Lucie County office; the org's main headquarters is in Vero Beach, Indian River County. Also runs the county's unaccompanied-adults "Housing Hub" program — see the GraceWay Village Family Shelter row's notes.
    https://tchelpspot.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • SafeSpace — 24-Hour Domestic Violence Hotline

    24-hour crisis line — confidential shelter location, no public address; pin shows Fort Pierce, the county seat
    (772) 464-4555
    24-hour crisis line; administrative office (772) 223-2399.

  • 211 (St. Lucie County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows Fort Pierce
    211 or (561) 383-1112
    24/7 free information and referral line covering Indian River, Martin, Okeechobee, Palm Beach, and St. Lucie counties — same Treasure Coast network already added for Okeechobee. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 19 counties running now.
    https://www.unitedwaymartin.org/211-helpline
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
