Shelter (overnight beds)

  • C.A.S.H. — Crestview Area Shelter For the Homeless

    120 Duggan Ave, Crestview, FL 32536
    (850) 634-6036
    Multi-service anchor for North Okaloosa County — street outreach, showers (last call 3pm), sack lunches, SNAP assistance, laundry, a clothing retail store, and a seasonal cold night shelter (activates below 40°F). See this map's separate C.A.S.H. food/hygiene/clothing entries at the same address.
    https://crestviewareashelter.org/

  • Opportunity Place, Inc. (OPI)

    305 Lovejoy Rd NW, Fort Walton Beach, FL 32548
    (850) 659-3190
    The only low-barrier emergency shelter in Okaloosa/Walton counties for families and single women — a genuinely walk-in-friendly option in a region where most other services are appointment-only.
    https://opifwb.org/

  • Shelter House of Northwest Florida

    16 Ferry Road SE, Fort Walton Beach, FL 32548 (program office; shelter location confidential)
    (850) 863-4777 (24-hour hotline) · 1-800-44ABUSE
    The only state-certified domestic and sexual violence center serving Okaloosa AND Walton counties — confidential shelter, court advocacy, transitional housing, child therapy, all free.
    https://www.shelterhousenwfl.org/

Coordinating agencies

  • Homelessness & Housing Alliance (HHA)

    Maintains offices in Fort Walton Beach and DeFuniak Springs — no single public storefront; pin shows Fort Walton Beach
    (850) 409-3070
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for Okaloosa AND Walton counties jointly — does not provide direct services itself, coordinates the network of providers listed on this map. Runs the HMIS database linking all Continuum agencies.
    https://hhalliance.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • 211 Northwest Florida (Okaloosa County)

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Fort Walton Beach, the county seat
    Dial 211 · text zip code to 898211 (M-F 9am-5pm)
    24/7 information and referral. This regional 211 network covers a very wide swath of the Panhandle — Bay, Calhoun, Escambia, Gulf, Holmes, Jackson, Okaloosa, Santa Rosa, Walton, and Washington counties — same org already on this map's Escambia and Santa Rosa entries.
    https://211nwfl.org/

  • Shelter House — 24-Hour Hotline

    Phone service — pin shows Fort Walton Beach, the county seat
    (850) 863-4777 · 1-800-44ABUSE
    24-hour domestic violence and sexual assault hotline serving Okaloosa and Walton counties — same org as this map's Shelter House shelter entry.
    https://www.shelterhousenwfl.org/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
