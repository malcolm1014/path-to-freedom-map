Legal public camping

  • Richloam Tract Primitive Camp Zones (Withlacoochee State Forest)

    Trailhead parking at the Richloam Fire Tower, Clay Sink Rd (~1 mile south of SR-50) — no street address; hike-in camp zones (South/East/North) are 3–4 miles from the parking area within Sumter County
    (352) 797-4100 (Withlacoochee State Forest Dispatch)
    Free, hike-in-only primitive camping (no water, no amenities at most zones) within the Richloam Tract, which spans Hernando, Pasco, Sumter, and Lake counties — these specific camp zones are sited in Sumter County. The only legal camping option found in this rural county this pass.
    https://www.fdacs.gov/Forest-Wildfire/Our-Forests/State-Forests/Withlacoochee-State-Forest
