Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Program REACH — Adopt-A-Family of the Palm Beaches

    1320 Henrietta Ave, West Palm Beach, FL 33401
    Palm Beach County's largest emergency shelter for families — up to 90 days of respite housing in 1-3 bedroom apartments, staffed 24/7.
    https://www.aafpbc.org/program-reach/

  • Senator Philip D. Lewis Homeless Resource Center

    1000 45th Street, West Palm Beach, FL 33407
    (561) 904-7900
    Palm Beach County's first homeless resource center (opened 2012) — centralized coordinated-entry point of access for individuals and families countywide.
    https://www.aafpbc.org/senator-philip-d-lewis-homeless-resource-center/

  • The Lord's Place

    2808 N Australian Avenue, West Palm Beach, FL 33407
    (561) 494-0125
    Emergency and supportive housing programs, plus meals served on-site (listed under Food Resources in the county's own resource guide).
    https://thelordsplace.org/

  • YWCA Harmony House

    1016 N. Dixie Highway, West Palm Beach, FL 33401
    (800) 973-9922
    72-bed secured, year-round, 24-hour shelter for women and children fleeing domestic abuse. Unlike most DV shelters in this project, multiple independent sources publish this street address (the facility is described as "secured" rather than confidential) — included as-is rather than generalized, since it's independently corroborated, not a single aggregator guess.
    https://ywcapbc.org/what-we-do/empowering-women/domestic-violence.html

  • Aid to Victims of Domestic Abuse (AVDA)

    Confidential — pin generalized to Delray Beach city center for resident safety
    (800) 355-8547 (24-hour hotline)
    Certified domestic violence center serving southern Palm Beach County — emergency shelter, transitional housing, advocacy, counseling. Services in English, Spanish, and ASL.
    https://www.avdaonline.org/

Legal public camping

  • John Prince Park Campground

    4759 Congress Avenue, Lake Worth Beach, FL 33461
    (561) 582-7992
    301-site county-run campground with full hookups on Lake Osborne. Reservations required, no same-day booking; camping capped at 155 days per 12-month period. URL updated — the old discover.pbc.gov domain is dead; www.pbcgov.org/parks/ itself now redirects to discover.pbcgov.org, confirming that's the real successor domain, though the whole discover.pbcgov.org platform was erroring for every page (not just this one) as of this check — call ahead if the site won't load.
    https://discover.pbcgov.org/parks/Pages/Camping-JohnPrincePark.aspx

  • 211 (Palm Beach County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows West Palm Beach
    211
    24/7 free information and referral line — same regional network already added for Okeechobee, St. Lucie, and Martin (Indian River/Martin/Okeechobee/Palm Beach/St. Lucie), branded here under its own Palm Beach-facing domain. Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 21 counties running now.
    https://211palmbeach.org/
