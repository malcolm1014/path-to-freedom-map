Shelter (overnight beds)

  • City Rescue Mission

    234 W State St, Jacksonville, FL 32201 (men — New Life Inn); women & women with children check in here and are transported to the Henriksen Women's Center, 426 S McDuff Ave, Jacksonville, FL 32254
    (904) 421-5161
    Overnight shelter for men (New Life Inn) and a separate campus for women/women with children (Henriksen Women's Center) — general population, not program-restricted.
    https://www.crmjax.org/

  • Trinity Rescue Mission

    622 W Union St, Jacksonville, FL 32202
    (904) 355-1205
    Overnight shelter, meals, residential recovery programs, transitional housing, and counseling for men and women. Open daily 9am-9pm. Known for accepting pets alongside their owners per multiple shelter directories — call ahead to confirm current pet policy.
    https://trinityrescue.org/

  • Salvation Army Northeast Florida — Red Shield Lodge

    900 W Adams St, Jacksonville, FL 32204
    (904) 356-8641
    24/7 emergency shelter — families can stay together up to a month at no cost; single women can stay up to 8 nights at no cost.
    https://jacksonville.salvationarmyflorida.org/nefl/duval-county

  • Hubbard House

    Confidential — pin generalized to Jacksonville city center for resident safety; mailing address PO Box 4909, Jacksonville, FL 32201
    (904) 354-3114 (24-hour hotline)
    Duval County's certified domestic violence center — emergency shelter, counseling, child anger-management classes. Participates in the state's Address Confidentiality Program. This project's Clay County entry already references this same org as an out-of-county resource.
    https://www.hubbardhouse.org/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Sulzbacher

    611 E Adams St, Jacksonville, FL 32202
    (904) 358-2411
    Northeast Florida's largest homeless services hub — emergency and long-term shelter, case management, showers, two meals daily, mailboxes, and job referrals. Mon-Fri 9am-4pm for general services; this project's Clay County entry already references this same org as an out-of-county fallback. Also runs an on-site Behavioral Health Clinic (psychiatrist, NP, therapists) in partnership with River Region Human Services, and a separate public day-center/shower program — see this project's Sulzbacher — Urban Rest Stop hygiene entry.
    https://www.sulzbacherjax.org/

  • Clara White Mission

    613 W Ashley St, Jacksonville, FL 32202
    (904) 354-4162
    Soup kitchen (breakfast 7 days/week 8:30-10am), food pantry (Mon/Thu 11:30am-1:30pm; Fri-Sun 8:30-10:30am), Meals 2 Go program, transitional housing, and vocational/job-training programs. Serves the general homeless population, no restriction found.
    https://clarawhitemission.org/

  • Mission House

    800 Shetter Ave, Jacksonville Beach, FL 32250
    (904) 241-6767
    Jacksonville Beach's only day center for people experiencing homelessness — 2 hot meals/day, showers, clothing, phones/computers/job board, an on-site medical clinic, and case management. Real geographic gap fill: every other Duval County entry on this map clusters in downtown Jacksonville, this is the first Jacksonville Beach entry.
    https://missionhousejax.org/

Mental & behavioral health

  • Mental Health Resource Center — North

    3333 West 20th Street, Jacksonville, FL 32254
    (904) 695-9145
    Duval County had zero mentalhealth-category entries before this addition, a real gap for one of Florida's largest cities. Covers the area west of the St. Johns River — 24-hour emergency evaluation and crisis stabilization (adult and child), inpatient psychiatric care, case management, medication management, outpatient therapy. Accepts Medicaid/Medicare and most insurance, plus an income-based sliding scale for the uninsured.
    https://www.mhrcflorida.com/services-locations.html

  • Mental Health Center of Jacksonville — Beach Blvd

    11820 Beach Blvd, Jacksonville, FL 32246
    (904) 642-9100
    24-hour emergency evaluation and crisis stabilization for children and adults, case management, medication management — the east-of-the-river counterpart to this project's Mental Health Resource Center — North entry above, possibly the same corporate entity operating under a location-specific name (unconfirmed by phone call). Accepts Medicaid/Medicare, income-based sliding scale available.
    https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/social-services/mental-health-services

  • Gateway Community Services — Main Campus

    555 Stockton Street, Jacksonville, FL 32204
    (904) 387-4661
    CARF-accredited substance-abuse and co-occurring mental health treatment — adult and adolescent outpatient, adult residential, detox. Four other Jacksonville-area sites exist (Broadway Ave, two Wally Green Recovery Campus units, Lexington Ave).
    https://gwjax.com/locations/

Legal public camping

  • Kathryn Abbey Hanna Park

    500 Wonderwood Dr, Jacksonville, FL 32233
    (904) 249-4700
    A city (not state) park with 300 campsites, real hot showers in multiple full bathhouses, laundry, camp store. Tent camping ~$18/night, RV ~$30/night; first-come-first-served. Tent max stay 14 nights per 45-day period, RV max 30 nights.
    https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/jaxparks/oceanfront-parks/kathryn-abbey-hanna-park/camping-at-hanna-park

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • 211 (United Way Northeast Florida)

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Jacksonville, the county seat
    Dial 211 · (904) 632-0600 · text "Hello" to 211904
    24/7 information and referral helpline, 100,000+ calls/year — also the region's accredited suicide intervention/prevention line. Same regional United Way org as this project's Clay County 211 entry.
    https://unitedwaynefl.org/get-help/

  • Baptist Behavioral Health — Crisis Hotline

    Phone-first crisis line — no single physical address; pin shows Jacksonville, the county seat
    (904) 202-7900 (24-hour, all ages) · text LIFE to 741741 · or dial 988
    24-hour behavioral-health crisis line, all ages, free regardless of insurance status. Baptist's own outpatient behavioral-health clinics are part of a private hospital system and sliding-scale/uninsured acceptance there is unconfirmed, which is why only the free crisis line is listed rather than the clinics themselves.
    https://www.baptistjax.com/services/behavioral-health
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
