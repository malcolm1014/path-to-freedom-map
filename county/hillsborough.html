Shelter (overnight beds)

  • The Salvation Army — Red Shield Lodge

    1603 N Florida Avenue, Tampa, FL 33602
    Emergency shelter for homeless adults.
    https://tampa.salvationarmyflorida.org

  • The Spring of Tampa Bay — Emergency Shelter

    Confidential location for resident safety — not publicly listed; pin shows downtown Tampa
    Domestic violence emergency shelter and services for Hillsborough County. Call 211 or the National DV Hotline for intake referral (see hotline category).
    https://thespring.org

  • Tampa Hope

    3704 E 3rd Avenue, Tampa, FL 33605
    Managed low-barrier outdoor shelter site ("Hope Cottages") for adults experiencing homelessness — three daily meals, clothing, toiletries, medical care, and life-skills classes. Also offers showers/laundry via its Matthew 25 Community Center (see hygiene category).
    https://www.ccdosp.org/tampa-hope/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Metropolitan Ministries — Tampa Campus

    2002 N Florida Avenue, Tampa, FL 33602
    (813) 209-1000
    Flagship multi-service campus for Hillsborough/Pinellas/Polk/Pasco — emergency and transitional shelter, food, financial assistance, showers, laundry, and computer/internet access. This is the Tampa main campus, a distinct site from this project's existing Pasco County Metropolitan Ministries entries (East Pasco and West Pasco MiraclePlace).
    https://www.metromin.org

  • Tampa Family Health Centers

    5707 N 22nd Street, Tampa, FL 33610
    (813) 239-8533
    Full primary medical care, with services specifically geared toward people experiencing homelessness; multiple additional locations across Hillsborough County.
    https://www.tfhc.org

Mental & behavioral health

  • ACTS Clubhouse

    201 E Yukon St, Tampa, FL 33604
    (813) 771-0958
    Clubhouse-model psychosocial rehabilitation program for adults with serious mental illness — members work side-by-side with staff on job training, culinary/tech skills, and employment support in a voluntary, no-time-limit community. Part of the Florida Clubhouse Coalition network. Call ahead or email clubhouse@actsfl.org to schedule a tour.
    https://www.actsfl.org/services/acts-clubhouse/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • The Spring of Tampa Bay — Crisis Hotline

    Confidential location — 24-hour crisis hotline; pin shows downtown Tampa
    24-hour domestic violence crisis hotline and victim services for Hillsborough County — call 211 or the National DV Hotline (1-800-799-7233) for direct routing.
    https://thespring.org

  • 211 (Hillsborough County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown Tampa
    211
    24/7 free information and referral line — same parent org family as First Contact (formerly 211 Tampa Bay Cares), already used for Hernando and Pinellas. Genuine gap fill — a large-metro county that had never gotten its own dedicated 211 row.
    https://www.crisiscenter.com/about-us/services-programs/gateway-contact-center/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
