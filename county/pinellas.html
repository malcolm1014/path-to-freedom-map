Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Catholic Charities — Pinellas Hope

    1213 16th St N, St Petersburg, FL 33705
    (727) 556-6397
    Emergency shelter for adults — one of the region's largest emergency-shelter campuses; also runs an on-site permanent supportive housing program (Pinellas Hope Apartments, 6363 9th Ave N) for those who complete the program.

  • Family Resources — SafePlace2B

    1615 Union St, Clearwater, FL 33755 (2nd site: 3761 5th Ave N, St Petersburg)
    (727) 298-1607 (Clearwater) · (727) 893-1893 (St Petersburg)
    Emergency shelter specifically for unaccompanied youth ages 10–17 — two sites across the county, pin shown is the Clearwater location.

  • Homeless Empowerment Program (HEP)

    1120 N Betty Lane, Clearwater, FL 33755
    (727) 442-9041
    Adult (18+) emergency shelter and supportive services — described by Pinellas County's own resource page as "the only social-services agency of its kind in Florida," serving singles, families, and veterans under one roof.
    https://www.hepempowers.org/services/

  • Pinellas Safe Harbor

    14840 49th St N, Clearwater, FL 33762
    (727) 464-8058
    Low-barrier emergency shelter (18+) run through the Sheriff's Office as a jail-diversion alternative — historically one of the more accessible same-night options for people who'd otherwise be arrested for quality-of-life offenses tied to homelessness.

  • Salvation Army — Emergency Shelter (St. Petersburg)

    310 14th Ave S, St Petersburg, FL 33705
    (727) 230-1578
    Emergency shelter for adults and families. Also serves a daily lunch (Salvation Army St. Petersburg meal program operates from this same campus, (727) 550-8080).

  • St. Vincent de Paul — C.A.R.E. Center of Hope

    401 15th St N, St Petersburg, FL 33713
    (727) 896-3300
    Emergency shelter for adults, families, and veterans. The org's separate shower-service building is one block away at 384 15th St N (see the Hygiene category).

  • WestCare — Turning Point

    1801 5th Ave N, St Petersburg, FL 33713
    (727) 823-7811
    Shelter specifically for people with substance-use issues, combining housing with treatment.

  • Community Action Stops Abuse (CASA)

    1011 1st Ave N, St Petersburg, FL 33705 (administrative/outreach office — actual shelter location is confidential for resident safety)
    (727) 895-4912
    Domestic-violence shelter and services. Same confidential-location pattern as this project's other DV shelters (Hernando's Dawn Center, Pasco's Sunrise, Citrus's CASA) — pin is the public-facing outreach office, not the safe house itself.

  • Hope Villages of America — The Haven

    Confidential — no public address; pin is Hope Villages' Clearwater campus area (700 Druid Rd) as a general reference point, not the safe house itself.
    (727) 442-4128
    Domestic-violence emergency safe house. Same confidential-location treatment as CASA above.

  • Alpha House

    701 5th Ave N, St Petersburg, FL 33701
    (727) 822-8190
    Transitional housing and support for pregnant women and new mothers.

  • Salvation Army — Hope Crest Transitional Living Center

    1521 E Druid Rd, Clearwater, FL 33756
    (727) 446-4177
    Transitional housing specifically for single mothers and their children.

  • Hope Villages of America — Goldsmith Gardens

    1552 South Myrtle Ave, Clearwater, FL 33756
    (727) 446-5964
    Emergency housing program.

Legal public camping

  • Fort De Soto Park Campground

    3500 Pinellas Bayway S, St Petersburg (Tierra Verde), FL 33715
    (727) 582-2100
    238 campsites, reservation-required, $33.90–$45.77/night depending on season and hookups. County residents can book 7 months ahead, non-residents 6 months ahead — the only county-run legal camping option found in this densely urban county.
    https://pinellas.gov/camping-information/

Coordinating agencies

  • Homeless Leadership Alliance of Pinellas (HLA)

    647 1st Ave N #2, St Petersburg, FL 33701
    Pinellas's official Continuum of Care lead agency, HMIS lead, and Coordinated Entry System manager since 1988 — coordination and system-planning body, not a direct-service walk-in site (30+ partner agencies feed into it). Publishes its own resource guide (pinellashomeless.org/resourceguide, updated March 2026) — the primary source for most of this county's entries.
    https://www.pinellashomeless.org

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • First Contact — Pinellas Homeless Helpline / 211

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows downtown St Petersburg
    Dial 211 or text 898211 · firstcontact.org/homeless-helpline
    Same parent organization as Hernando County's First Contact entry (formerly 211 Tampa Bay Cares) but this is Pinellas's dedicated homeless-helpline line — staffed by homeless-system experts, not a generic 211 operator. Best first call for anyone unsure where to start in this county.

  • Bay Area Legal Services — Clearwater

    800 Drew St, Clearwater, FL 33755 (2nd site: 4948 Central Ave, St Petersburg)
    1-800-625-2257
    Free civil legal aid — eviction defense, family law, disability benefits, consumer matters. Same statewide org as Hernando's entry, this is Pinellas's own regional office pair.
    https://www.bals.org

  • Gulfcoast Legal Services

    501 1st Ave N Ste 420, St Petersburg, FL 33701
    (727) 821-0726
    Civil legal aid; also runs the Pinellas Eviction Diversion Program from the same building (Suite 519, (727) 582-7475).

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline

    National 24/7 hotline — no physical location; pin shows downtown St Petersburg
    1-800-799-7233
    24/7 confidential support for domestic-violence survivors.

  • SAMHSA National Helpline

    National 24/7 hotline — no physical location; pin shows downtown St Petersburg
    1-800-662-4357
    Free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral for substance-use and mental-health issues.
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
