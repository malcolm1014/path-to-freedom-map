Coordinating agencies

  • Big Bend Continuum of Care (Franklin County)

    2507 Callaway Road, Suite 102A, Tallahassee, FL 32303 (physically located in Leon County)
    (850) 792-5015
    HUD-designated Continuum of Care lead agency for the 8-county Big Bend region: Franklin, Gadsden, Jefferson, Leon, Liberty, Madison, Taylor, and Wakulla — a genuinely different CoC from Doorways of NW Florida, which this map's western Panhandle counties use. Coordinates policy/strategy rather than providing direct services.
    https://bigbendcoc.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • 211 Big Bend (Franklin County)

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Apalachicola, the county seat
    Dial 211 · (850) 617-6333 · text zip code to 898211
    24/7 crisis counseling and information/referral for all 8 Big Bend counties (Franklin, Gadsden, Jefferson, Leon, Liberty, Madison, Taylor, Wakulla) — a DIFFERENT 211 network from 211 Northwest Florida, which covers this map's Panhandle counties in the Doorways region. No eligibility criteria to call, doesn't require being in crisis.
    https://211bigbend.org/
