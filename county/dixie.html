Legal public camping

  • Butler-Douglas Memorial Park (Ed Butler Memorial County Park)

    244 8th Ave W, Horseshoe Beach, FL 32648
    County-run waterfront camping and boat ramp on the Gulf. County residents: $6/night tent, $10/night tent w/ electric or RV. Non-residents: $10/night tent, $20/night tent w/ electric or RV. Also has public restrooms and showers on-site (see Hygiene category).
