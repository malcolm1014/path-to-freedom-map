Shelter (overnight beds)

  • The Matrix Community Outreach Center

    37 North 9th Street, DeFuniak Springs, FL 32433
    (850) 892-1090
    North Walton County's main homelessness-services anchor — seasonal cold night shelter, food pantry, homelessness prevention, and needs assessments.
    https://www.matrixcoc.org/

  • Shelter House of Northwest Florida — Walton County

    16 Ferry Road SE, Fort Walton Beach, FL 32548 (physically located in Okaloosa County; shelter location confidential)
    (850) 863-4777 (24-hour hotline) · 1-800-44ABUSE
    The only state-certified domestic and sexual violence center serving Okaloosa AND Walton counties — same org already on this map's Okaloosa County entry.
    https://www.shelterhousenwfl.org/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Caring and Sharing of South Walton

    112 Lynn Dr, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459
    (850) 267-2866
    South Walton's main multi-service hub — food pantry (walk-in Mon/Wed/Fri 9am-2pm), clothing vouchers, financial assistance, case management, and SNAP/Medicaid application help. Counseling/assessments by appointment. Real geographic complement to the Matrix Community Outreach Center in North Walton (DeFuniak Springs).
    https://caringandsharingsowal.org/

Government benefits offices

  • JobsPlus One Stop Career Center — ACCESS Point

    171 North 9th Street, DeFuniak Springs, FL 32433
    (850) 892-8668
    Local ACCESS Florida application-assistance point for SNAP/Medicaid/cash assistance. No full DCF walk-in service center exists physically in Walton County — the nearest is the Fort Walton Beach ACCESS Storefront in Okaloosa County (already on this map).

Transportation

  • Walton County Transportation (GoWal)

    194 N 6th Street, DeFuniak Springs, FL 32433
    (850) 892-2422
    GoWal is a FREE fixed-route shuttle Mon-Fri 6am-6:30pm along US-331 connecting DeFuniak Springs to South Walton (via Freeport) — no fare at all. The same office also runs a non-emergency Community Transportation Program (medical, shopping, education, employment trips) for eligible riders.
    https://gowaltransit.com/

Pet food pantries

  • Walton County Animal Shelter

    365 Triple G Road, DeFuniak Springs, FL 32433
    (850) 892-8758
    Low-cost pet adoption ($25, includes spay/neuter, vaccines, microchip). Tue-Thu 9am-4pm, Fri-Sat 10am-3pm. NOTE: the separate nonprofit Humane Society of Walton County has closed — this county-run shelter is the sole remaining option.
    https://waltonso.org/animal-services/

Legal public camping

  • Topsail Hill Preserve State Park

    7525 W County Highway 30A, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459
    (850) 267-8330
    156 resort-style RV sites plus 22 tent sites, with two large climate-controlled bathhouses with real showers (a separate shower-equipped restroom serves the tent area too). Also has a pool, laundry, and camp store.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/topsail-hill-preserve-state-park
