Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Another Way Shelter — Live Oak Office (Suwannee County)

    100 Court St SE #202, Live Oak, FL 32064 (outreach office — actual shelter location confidential for resident safety)
    (386) 208-0552 · Hotline (866) 875-7983
    Domestic violence, sexual violence, human trafficking, and rape crisis services for male and female victims and their dependent children — serves Columbia, Hamilton, Suwannee, and Lafayette counties. Unlike this project's Levy/Gilchrist/Dixie entries for the same org (which had no confirmed physical presence and used a generalized county-seat pin), this one has a real, confirmed Live Oak outreach office address.
    https://anotherwayinc.org

    1365 Ohio Ave N, Live Oak, FL 32064 (administrative office — actual shelter location confidential for resident safety)
    (386) 364-2100 · DV Hotline (800) 500-1119 · TTY (386) 364-1334
