Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Kearney Center

    2650 Municipal Way, Tallahassee, FL 32304
    (850) 792-9000
    The Big Bend's main emergency shelter for single adults — hot meals, real on-site showers, laundry, and on-site healthcare access, all at one campus.
    https://kearneycenter.org/

  • Family Promise — Hope Community

    2729 W Pensacola St, Tallahassee, FL 32304
    (850) 576-5566
    Emergency shelter for families (any single adult with legal custody of a minor child) — three meals a day, case management, housing placement, childcare/healthcare connections, transportation assistance. Also runs Home Front veteran housing (1602 Stuckey Ave) and SSVF case management for veterans.
    https://familypromisebigbend.org/

  • Capital City Youth Services (CCYS)

    2407 Roberts Ave, Tallahassee, FL 32310
    (850) 576-6000
    Emergency shelter, counseling, and transitional housing for youth under 24.
    https://ccys.org/

  • Refuge House

    PO Box 20910, Tallahassee, FL 32316 (emergency shelter location confidential)
    (850) 681-2111 (24-hour hotline)
    The Big Bend's certified domestic and sexual violence center — Leon County is its primary service area (along with Taylor County), extending to Franklin/Gadsden/Jefferson/Liberty/Madison/Wakulla too. This IS the org's real headquarters county — already used as cross-county reference coordinates for Franklin/Wakulla, now gets its own native Leon-tagged row.
    https://refugehouse.com/
