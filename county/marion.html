Multi-service anchor centers

  • Interfaith Emergency Services (IES)

    435 NW 2nd St, Ocala, FL 34475
    (352) 629-8868
    Marion County's largest multi-service hub: emergency homeless shelter, a food pantry (Interfaith operates 6 pantries countywide from this hub), clothing closet, shower ministries, the Food 4 Kids backpack program, hygiene supplies, diapers, ID cards, work boots, reading glasses, and gas vouchers for medical appointments. Open Mon–Fri 9am–4pm. The single best first call in this county alongside the Joint Office of Homelessness.
    https://www.iesmarion.org

  • Silver Springs Shores Resource Center

    9264 SE Maricamp Rd, Ocala, FL 34472
    (352) 687-0350
    Community resource center serving the Silver Springs Shores area (southeast Ocala) — economic empowerment and general community services, an important geographic anchor since most other entries this pass cluster in central Ocala.
    http://www.shoresresource.org

Legal public camping

  • Juniper Springs Recreation Area (Ocala National Forest)

    26701 FL-40, Silver Springs, FL 34488
    1-877-444-6777 (reservations)
    78 developed campsites, ~$37/night (2025 rate) plus a $14.50/person day-use fee; reservations via Recreation.gov up to 6 months ahead. Not a free option, but the most accessible legal camping option within Marion County's portion of Ocala National Forest.
    https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234031

Coordinating agencies

  • Ocala/Marion Joint Office of Homelessness Prevention

    Coordinated Entry Access Point: 201 SE 3rd St, 2nd Floor, Ocala, FL 34471
    (352) 671-8770
    Marion County's Continuum of Care lead — a joint City of Ocala/Marion County office; Ocala staff run outreach, Coordinated Entry, and HMIS, while County staff oversee funding/grant applications. The CoC Manager's office is separately at 2710 E. Silver Springs Blvd, (352) 671-8775. Not a direct-service walk-in pantry/shelter — this is the coordination and system-planning body, same role as this project's other counties' coalition entries.
