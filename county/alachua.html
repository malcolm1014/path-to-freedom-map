Shelter (overnight beds)

  • GRACE Marketplace — Emergency Shelter

    3055 NE 28th Drive, Gainesville, FL 32609
    (352) 792-0800
    184 beds across 4 general-population programs (men, women, couples, veterans) — Alachua County's main general-population emergency shelter, filling a real gap where this map's only prior shelter entries were DV-specific (Peaceful Paths, Family Promise). Bed entries assigned daily at 3:30pm; guests must be present to confirm a bed. Same campus as the hub/hygiene entries already on this map, split into its own row since general shelter capacity is the category most people search this map for.
    https://www.gracemarketplace.org/emergency-shelter

  • Peaceful Paths Domestic Abuse Network

    2100 Northwest 53rd Avenue, Gainesville, FL 32653 (administrative office — actual shelter location confidential for resident safety)
    (352) 377-5690 (admin) · Crisis line (352) 377-8255
    Domestic-violence shelter and services — confirmed service area is Alachua, Bradford, and Union counties. This project's earlier Dixie County entry for this same org flagged an honest caveat that Dixie itself isn't in its confirmed service area; Alachua is the county Peaceful Paths actually serves directly.
    https://www.domesticshelters.org/help/fl/gainesville/32653/peaceful-paths-domestic-abuse-network

  • Family Promise of Gainesville

    229 SW 5th St, Gainesville, FL 32601
    (352) 378-2030
    Day shelter, meals, and social services for families with children experiencing homelessness. This project's earlier Dixie County entry for this org used a PO-box-only address since no physical location was confirmed at the time — this is the org's real day-shelter address, now confirmed.
    https://www.familypromisegvl.org

Food pantries & meals

  • GRACE Marketplace — Meals

    3055 NE 28th Drive, Gainesville, FL 32609
    (352) 792-0800
    Free breakfast and dinner served daily on campus, open to anyone in need (not limited to shelter guests) — a real second food option alongside Bread of the Mighty Food Bank, which primarily distributes groceries rather than serving prepared meals.

    325 NW 10th Ave, Gainesville, FL 32601
    Regional food bank distributing to partner pantries across North Central Florida.

Multi-service anchor centers

  • GRACE Marketplace

    3055 NE 28th Drive, Gainesville, FL 32609
    (352) 792-0800
    Alachua County's one-stop homeless resource center — safe emergency shelter, permanent housing placement, hot meals, storage, case management, job training, and space for partner health-care providers. Showers open to the public 7am–7pm daily (see Hygiene category). On RTS bus routes 25A and 26. The most comprehensive single hub found anywhere in this project so far.
    https://www.gracemarketplace.org

Coordinating agencies

  • TaskForce for Ending Homelessness / Keys to Home (Alachua County)

    Regional CoC lead — confirmed NO physical Gainesville office (mailing address is a Fort Lauderdale PO Box); pin shows Gainesville, the county seat
    Official HUD Continuum of Care lead agency for the "Keys to Home" FL-508 region (Alachua, Bradford, Dixie, Gilchrist, Levy, Putnam, Union counties) — same org as this project's Levy/Gilchrist/Dixie entries. Alachua is the region's largest county and namesake city (Gainesville), but even here TaskForce has no confirmed local office — its own contact page lists only a Broward County PO Box, directing North Central Florida residents to call 211 instead.
    https://keystohome.org
