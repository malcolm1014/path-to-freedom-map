Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Quigley House

    PO Box 8219, Fleming Island, FL 32006 (confidential — actual shelter location not publicly listed for resident safety; pin shows Green Cove Springs, the county seat)
    (904) 284-0061 (24-hour hotline) · (904) 284-0340 (office)
    Comprehensive domestic violence and sexual assault center for Clay County — 24-hour hotline, safe shelter (women, men, children, elderly, and LGBTQ welcome), individual/group/child counseling, case management, transitional housing, and a pet shelter (an unusually complete service list for a DV shelter in this project).
    https://www.quigleyhouse.org

  • Hubbard House (out of county, Jacksonville)

    6629 Beach Blvd, Jacksonville, FL 32216 (physically located in Duval County)
    24-Hour Hotline (904) 354-3114 · 24-Hour Textline (904) 210-3698
    A second domestic-violence shelter option referenced for Clay County residents — emergency housing for abused women and children, counseling, child anger-management classes.
    https://www.hubbardhouse.org

Legal public camping

  • Mike Roess Gold Head Branch State Park

    6239 SR 21, Keystone Heights, FL 32656
    (352) 473-4701
    Six miles north of Keystone Heights on SR 21. Camping ~$20/night plus a $6.70 reservation fee ($7/night RV utility fee doesn't apply to tents); cabins ~$65/night. Physically located in Clay County — this project's Bradford County entry used this same park as an out-of-county fallback; this is its actual home county.
