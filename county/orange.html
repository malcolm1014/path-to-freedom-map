Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Coalition for the Homeless of Central Florida

    18 N Terry Avenue, Orlando, FL 32801
    (407) 652-5300
    Low-barrier residential shelter focused on housing, emergency services, and diversion; serves meals daily at 6:45pm to anyone in need, no ID required. Near the free LYMMO Grapefruit bus line. Distinct from the Homeless Services Network of Central Florida (the region's CoC lead agency, also in this project's data).
    https://www.centralfloridahomeless.org

  • Orlando Union Rescue Mission — Project Hope (Men)

    3300 W Colonial Drive, Orlando, FL 32808
    (407) 423-3596
    Shelter for single men; 3 meals a day, 365 days a year.
    https://www.ourm.org

  • Orlando Union Rescue Mission — Family Home

    1525 W Washington Street, Orlando, FL 32805
    (407) 423-2131
    Shelter for women and families; 3 meals a day, 365 days a year.
    https://www.ourm.org

  • Harbor House of Central Florida — Administrative Office

    708 E Colonial Drive, Suite 201, Orlando, FL 32803 (administrative office — the actual emergency shelter location is confidential for resident safety)
    Domestic violence shelter and services for Orange County; call the hotline (see hotline category) for intake — the residential shelter's street address is not published.
    https://www.harborhousefl.com

Clinics & health centers

  • Health Care Center for the Homeless

    232 N Orange Blossom Trail, Orlando, FL 32805
    Full primary medical and dental care specifically for people experiencing homelessness; also runs the H.O.P.E. street outreach program.
    https://hcch.org

  • Orange Blossom Family Health

    232 N Orange Blossom Trail, Orlando, FL 32805
    (407) 428-5011
    Full-scope family health center — medical, dental, behavioral health, pharmacy, vision, urgent care, on-site lab — sliding-scale fees for uninsured patients, not limited to those experiencing homelessness (broader eligibility than the Health Care Center for the Homeless entry already on this map at the same address, its parent org). A real second medical option for low-income/uninsured residents who aren't literally unhoused.
    https://obfh.org

Legal public camping

  • Wekiwa Springs State Park (Orange County)

    1800 Wekiwa Circle, Apopka, FL 32712
    (407) 553-4383
    60 wooded campsites with water/electric hookups, plus two primitive canoe-access sites (Otter Camp, Big Buck Camp). This is the park's actual home county — this project's Seminole County data references this same park as an out-of-county fallback, since the Wekiva River corridor is closely associated with Seminole even though the park entrance itself is in Orange County.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/wekiwa-springs-state-park

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • Harbor House of Central Florida — 24-Hour DV Hotline

    708 E Colonial Drive, Suite 201, Orlando, FL 32803 (administrative office — hotline is 24/7 regardless of office hours)
    24-hour domestic violence crisis hotline, safety planning, and shelter intake for Orange County.
    https://www.harborhousefl.com

  • 211 (Orange County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown Orlando
    211 or (407) 835-0900
    24/7 free information and referral line, Florida's largest 211 crisis contact center — covers Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties (same network already added for Seminole). Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught in St. Johns, Flagler, Volusia, and Seminole.
    https://www.hfuw.org/
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
