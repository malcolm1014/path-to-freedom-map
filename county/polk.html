Legal public camping

  • Lake Kissimmee State Park (Polk County)

    14248 Camp Mack Road, Lake Wales, FL 33898
    (863) 696-1112
    This park's own campground facility is physically in Polk County — this is its native home county. This project's Osceola County data references this same park as an out-of-county fallback, since Lake Kissimmee itself (the body of water) is almost entirely within Osceola even though the park entrance/campground sits on the Polk side. 60 campsites with water/electric hookups.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/lake-kissimmee-state-park

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • Peace River Center — Domestic Violence Hotline

    Confidential location — 24-hour crisis hotline; pin shows downtown Lakeland
    (863) 413-2700
    24-hour domestic violence crisis hotline and victim services for Polk County.
    https://www.peacerivercenter.org

  • 211 (Polk County)

    Countywide phone service — pin shows downtown Lakeland
    211 or 1-888-370-7188
    24/7 free information and referral line covering Polk, Hardee, and Highlands counties — a DIFFERENT 211 network than the Heart of Florida United Way one used for the Orlando-metro counties (Orange/Osceola/Seminole). Genuine gap fill — same missing-211 pattern caught 7 counties running now.
    https://uwcf.org/211
Part of Path to Freedom — verify hours/services before visiting; in immediate danger, call 911. Not affiliated with any organization listed.
