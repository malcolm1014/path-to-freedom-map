Shelter (overnight beds)

  • William M. Neece Center (FKOC Men's Emergency Shelter)

    2221 Patterson Ave, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 293-0641
    24-bed emergency shelter for men, dormitory-style, with a structured 90-day program to help secure employment, manage sobriety, and build an individual recovery plan. Same campus as this map's FKOC Loaves & Fish Food Pantry entry.
    https://fkoc.org/housing/

  • Domestic Abuse Shelter of the Florida Keys (DAS)

    Confidential — pin generalized to Key West for resident safety
    (305) 743-4440 (24-hour hotline)
    Countywide emergency housing, crisis intervention, and case management for domestic violence survivors — covers the entire Keys, not just Key West. Office line (305) 743-5452.
    https://www.domesticabuseshelter.org/

  • Florida Keys Children's Shelter

    73 High Point Road, Tavernier, FL 33070
    (305) 852-4246 · 1-800-RUNAWAY
    19-bed runaway/homeless youth shelter, open 24/7/365 — counseling, reunification services, and the only street-outreach program for homeless/runaway youth in the Florida Keys. Also operates Key West group homes.
    https://www.fkcs.org/

Food pantries & meals

  • FKOC — Loaves & Fish Food Pantry

    2221 Patterson Ave, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 295-7580
    Food distribution Mon-Fri 9am-4pm — same campus as this map's William M. Neece Center shelter entry, not a separate site.
    https://fkoc.org/food-pantry/

  • Keys Area Interdenominational Resources (KAIR)

    3010 Overseas Highway, Marathon, FL 33050
    (305) 743-4582
    Certified food pantry distributing ~500,000 lbs of food/year, helping 30-40 families/day, plus daily brown-bag lunches. Open to anyone, Mon-Fri 9am-12pm (or by appointment) — the Middle Keys' main food resource, geographically distinct from FKOC's Key West and Upper Keys sites.
    https://kaironline.org/

  • First Baptist Church of Key Largo

    99001 Overseas Highway, Key Largo, FL 33037
    (305) 451-2265
    Emergency food assistance and a weekly community dinner (Samaritan Supper, Wed 5pm) — covers the very northern end of the Keys chain, the county's most distant point from Key West.
    https://fbckl.com/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Florida Keys Outreach Coalition (FKOC)

    1434 Kennedy Drive, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 293-4370
    Main administrative office — shelter and multi-stage housing programs, homelessness prevention, food pantry, and supportive services. Serves all of Monroe County. Note: the org has been rebranding toward the name "Keys Health + Housing" — verify current branding before a visit.
    https://fkoc.org/

  • FKOC — Upper Keys Prevention & Outreach Office

    Coral Isles Church, 90001 Overseas Highway, Tavernier, FL 33070
    (305) 916-5454
    Upper Keys satellite office for FKOC's financial-crisis support, referrals, and outreach — important for Key Largo/Tavernier/Islamorada residents, since FKOC's shelter and main hub sit ~70 miles south in Key West.
    https://fkoc.org/

Clinics & health centers

  • Florida Department of Health in Monroe County (Gato Building)

    1100 Simonton Street, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 293-7500
    HIV/TB testing, immunizations, environmental health. Also has satellite clinics in Marathon ((305) 676-3975) and Tavernier ((305) 676-3933) covering the rest of the Keys.
    https://monroe.floridahealth.gov/

  • Lower Keys Medical Center

    5900 College Road, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 294-5531
    The Lower Keys' acute-care hospital with a 24/7 emergency department — the nearest full hospital for Key West and the Lower Keys (Marathon and the Upper Keys are ~45+ and ~90+ miles away respectively).
    https://www.lkmc.com/

Clothing & thrift vouchers

  • First Baptist Church of Islamorada

    81201 Overseas Highway, Islamorada, FL 33036
    (305) 664-4910
    Clothing closet plus a food pantry and weekly community dinner — no dedicated standalone clothing/thrift resource was found elsewhere in the Keys, so this dual-purpose church program fills the category.
    https://fbcislamorada.org/

Free computers & workspace

  • Monroe County Public Library — Key West

    700 Fleming Street, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 292-3595
    Free internet, computer access, physical/digital collections. Main branch of a countywide system with additional locations in Big Pine ((305) 872-0992), Marathon ((305) 743-5156), Islamorada ((305) 664-4645), and Key Largo — a real, spread-out branch network covering the whole island chain.
    https://www.keyslibraries.org/locations/key-west-library

  • Monroe County Public Library — Marathon

    3490 Overseas Highway, Marathon, FL 33050
    (305) 743-5156
    Free internet/computer access — a real dedicated row for the Middle Keys, geographically meaningful given the ~100-mile spread of Monroe County between Key West and Key Largo. Same system as the Key West main branch already on this map.
    https://www.keyslibraries.org/locations/marathon-library

Job centers

  • CareerSource South Florida — Key West Center

    1111 12th Street, Suite 307-308, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 292-6762
    Resume writing, job seeking, networking, and job-readiness skills. Also has a Key Largo location ((305) 853-3540). CONFIDENCE NOTE: a different org, "CareerSource Southwest Florida," has its own unrelated Key West office in the same building (Suite 312) — verified this entry against CareerSource South Florida's own site to avoid conflating the two similarly-named orgs.
    https://www.careersourcesfl.com/

Government benefits offices

  • Monroe County Social Services

    1100 Simonton St, Suite 1-190, Key West, FL 33040
    (305) 292-4408
    Limited rent/utility assistance, prescription drug assistance, burial/cremation assistance. Mon-Fri 8am-5pm. Also reachable in Tavernier at (305) 852-7125 for the Upper Keys.
    https://www.monroecounty-fl.gov/socialservices

Transportation

  • Monroe County Transit

    Countywide service — no single fixed office address found; pin shows Key West, the county seat
    (305) 292-4424
    Senior and disability-related transportation countywide. Other Keys-specific transportation options include Key West's Rides On-Demand bus service and the Dade-Monroe Express (Miami-Dade Transit) connecting Florida City to Marathon.
    https://www.monroecounty-fl.gov/142/Transportation

Pet food pantries

  • Florida Keys SPCA — Marathon Campus

    10550 Aviation Boulevard, Marathon, FL 33050
    (305) 743-4800
    Low-cost and free spay/neuter services, adoption, and humane-law-enforcement partnership. A second campus has operated the Key West animal shelter on Stock Island since 1999 — call ahead to confirm which location can help with pet-food or care needs.
    https://fkspca.org/

Legal public camping

  • Bahia Honda State Park

    36850 Overseas Highway, Big Pine Key, FL 33043
    (305) 872-2353
    Real hot showers at the Buttonwood Campground bathhouse (also usable by Bayside campers, a half-mile away). One of the most in-demand campgrounds in the state park system — reserve well ahead.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/bahia-honda-state-park

Coordinating agencies

  • Monroe County Homeless Services Continuum-of-Care

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Key West, the county seat
    (305) 440-2315
    Countywide lead agency coordinating the CoC process for the entire Florida Keys, from Key Largo to Key West.
    https://www.monroehomelesscoc.org/

Hotlines & crisis lines

  • 211 of Miami-Dade and Monroe

    Phone/online service — no single physical address; pin shows Key West, the county seat
    Dial 211 · (855) 883-4429
    Information and referral to statewide resources — suicide prevention, food banks, shelters, legal assistance. Monroe's 211 network is run jointly with Miami-Dade, a different regional pairing than any other county on this map so far.
    https://www.211miami.org/

  • Domestic Abuse Shelter of the Florida Keys — 24-Hour Hotline

    Confidential — pin generalized to Key West for resident safety
    (305) 743-4440 · Text (305) 240-0105
    Crisis intervention and case management, 24/7, countywide — same org as this map's DAS shelter entry.
    https://www.domesticabuseshelter.org/
