Shelter (overnight beds)

  • Nassau County Cold Night Shelter

    MLK Recreation Center, 1200 Elm Street, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 556-7340
    Seasonal, general-population emergency shelter — cots and donated restaurant meals, open November through March on nights the temperature drops to 42°F or below. Not a year-round resource; call the Coalition to confirm nightly activation.
    https://www.chnassau.org/gethelp

  • Salvation Army Nassau County — Hope House

    410 South 8th Street, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 321-0435
    Food bank, clothing, hot meals, and financial aid. Also hosts the Coalition's Saturday breakfast/lunch service (see Day Drop-In Center entry). Call to confirm current shelter-bed availability alongside its food/financial-aid programs.
    https://jacksonville.salvationarmyflorida.org/nefl/nassau-county

  • Micah's Place

    Confidential — pin generalized to Fernandina Beach city center for resident safety
    (904) 225-9979 (24-hour hotline)
    The only certified domestic violence center serving Nassau County — shelter, advocacy, counseling, legal advocacy, and support groups, all free and confidential.
    https://www.micahsplace.org/

Multi-service anchor centers

  • Coalition for the Homeless of Nassau County — Day Drop-In Center

    1005 South 14th Street, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034 (Fernandina Beach Church of Christ)
    (904) 556-7340
    Hot breakfast and lunch, showers, laundry, mail service, clothing, personal-care supplies, and benefits-application help. Open 9-11am, 5 days/week; Saturday breakfast/lunch relocates to the Salvation Army Hope House instead.
    https://www.chnassau.org/gethelp

  • Barnabas Center

    1303 Jasmine Street, Suite 101, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 261-7000
    Countywide human-services hub — food pantry plus mobile food distributions (5x/month across Nassau County), free/low-cost medical and dental care, crisis financial assistance (rent/utility), budgeting workshops, and the New to You thrift store (see clothing entry). Mon-Thu 9am-4pm.
    https://barnabasnassau.org/

Government benefits offices

  • Barnabas Center — Crisis Financial Assistance

    1303 Jasmine Street, Suite 101, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 261-7000
    Rent, utility, and other crisis financial assistance, plus budgeting/financial-education workshops — same site as this map's Barnabas Center hub entry. No walk-in DCF ACCESS office was found physically located in Nassau County; the nearest is the Gateway Service Center in Jacksonville (Duval County, already on this map) — apply online at myflfamilies.com or call ahead before a trip.
    https://barnabasnassau.org/

Transportation

  • NassauTRANSIT

    102 N 13th Street, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 261-0700
    Fixed-route bus service (Hilliard to Fernandina Beach, plus a River City Marketplace/downtown Jacksonville connection) and registered paratransit. Nassau Express Select offers a JTA-partnered weekday commuter run to downtown Jacksonville. Note: the county's Transportation Disadvantaged (TD) program transitioned from the Council on Aging to JTA on Oct 1, 2024 — this fixed-route/paratransit line is still the right first call either way.
    https://nassautransit.org/

Legal public camping

  • Fort Clinch State Park

    2601 Atlantic Avenue, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 277-7274
    69 campsites across two campgrounds (Amelia River and Atlantic Beach) — real hot showers, restrooms, laundry, dump station. Reservations required, up to 11 months in advance.
    https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/fort-clinch-state-park

Coordinating agencies

  • Coalition for the Homeless of Nassau County

    1005 South 14th Street, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
    (904) 556-7340
    Lead homelessness-services coordinating org for Nassau County — shelter, food, housing support, and case management, run out of the Fernandina Beach Church of Christ. Also directly operates the Day Drop-In Center and the seasonal Cold Night Shelter (see those entries).
